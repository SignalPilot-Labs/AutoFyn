# Proof-Outliner Per-Role Rules

ALWAYS: When explorers discover a key geometric point (like A' here), define it explicitly in multiple equivalent ways (reflection, intersection of two lines, coordinate formula) to give builders flexibility in the proof. (round 1)

ALWAYS: For geometry problems with symmetry targets (OM = ON), reduce to a concyclicity or fixed-point claim immediately - the symmetry usually follows trivially from the deeper structure. (round 1)

ALWAYS: Read all explorer reports carefully for numerical dead ends (like BKLN not concyclic) and exclude them from the approach skeletons. (round 1)

ALWAYS: When all approaches share the same gap for 2+ rounds, open at least one NEW approach that attacks the gap from a different angle (like inversion transforming concyclicity to collinearity) rather than only advancing the existing approaches. (round 3)

ALWAYS: When explorers find explicit algebraic constraints (like the trig equations * and **), incorporate them into the skeleton as named steps that builders can use. These close the system and make algebraic approaches tractable. (round 3)
