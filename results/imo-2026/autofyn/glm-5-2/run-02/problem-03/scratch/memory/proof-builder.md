# proof-builder role memory

ALWAYS: test the load-bearing claim of a variational/transfer engine on small-n brute force (exact rational arithmetic, `fractions.Fraction`) BEFORE attempting a proof — the dispatch's "highest risk" engines often die on a concrete n=3 counterexample, and a falsified engine must be reported honestly, not papered over (round 3, pairing-partner-transfer: two-tail cancellation falsified on 21/33 n=3 minimizers).

ALWAYS: when an engine is dead, still harvest any proved sub-lemmas it produced along the way (e.g. CK one-line lemma) and propose them for certification — the round is not a total loss (round 3).

NEVER: fake a tail-cancellation or monovariant claim the brute force contradicts — a dead-end-with-reason is a valid population member; overclaiming is worse (round 3).

NEVER: restrict a "permissive transfer" test to only the canonical move choice — test the MOST permissive version (any removal + any re-placement) before declaring an engine dead, so the verdict is robust to "the transfer was just suboptimal" objections (round 3).

NEVER: re-dispatch a falsified variational engine (2-piece transfer / two-tail cancellation for G1 of imo-2026-03) — it hits the same certified −2T tail-flip wall as per-mark induction (round 3).

ALWAYS: when a dispatch hands you a recursive strategy engine (e.g. greedy R-pile) with a claimed small-n verification, RE-RUN the verification yourself on the FULL config space (grid sweep of dominant + balanced + extreme-dominant), not just the explorer's cherry-picked config — the explorer's "A=0 after 2 marks on (.6,.2,.1,.1)" did not generalize; 42/86 dominant n=3 configs failed, plus all balanced configs (round 3, two-regime-disjunctive).

ALWAYS: distinguish "the invariant failed" from "the strategy is suboptimal" when an engine fails — the reviewer may ask you to prove an invariant (e.g. consecutive-rank) that is actually MOOT; test whether the failure is interleaving (invariant) or bad cut selection (suboptimality) before investing in the invariant proof (round 3, two-regime-disjunctive: greedy failure was suboptimality, the consecutive-rank invariant was moot).

NEVER: re-dispatch the greedy R-pile engine (cut a_2 out of a_1) for regime-N of imo-2026-03 — it is RULED OUT by counterexamples (dyadic overshoot characterized by the dyadic-ratio lemma; balanced and extreme-dominant non-dyadic configs overshoot). The regime-N gap needs a different framing (multiplicity-parity / even-block is the candidate, unproven) (round 3).

ALWAYS: when a weight-function approach fails to find an explicit w, try the EQUIVALENCE-REDUCTION rescue — instead of constructing w with A ≥ Σw ≥ α, prove the target inequality is EQUIVALENT to a cleaner inequality on a natural decomposition (for imo-2026-03 Lemma L, the M⊎R merge reduces L(n+1) to the single inequality `e_M ≤ o_R` between two sub-sums of the merged sort — this localizes the obstruction without finding w). (round 3, imo-2026-03)
ALWAYS: for cake/partition alternating-sum bounds, try the PARITY argument on the natural integer grid — scale by the total; if the total is ODD, the pair-excesses e_i = q_{2i−1}−q_{2i} satisfy Σe_i ≡ total (mod 2), forcing Σe_i ≥ 1 (an odd non-negative integer). This closes grid-aligned refinements of imo-2026-03 Lemma L in ONE line; it does NOT lift to reals (finer odd grid gives weaker bound) but is a genuine rigorous result for the restricted class and reveals the mechanism. (round 3, imo-2026-03)
NEVER: assume conjecture (S) "smallest piece ≥ α(n) at the minimizer" for real-valued cake games — it is FALSE for reals (Xiang can split a piece to make a sub-α fragment); the fragment cancels at an odd rank so A ≥ α survives, but the odd-count cheap-kill CK cannot detect it. CK only closes the GRID case (where pieces are positive multiples of α = 1/D(n), so smallest ≥ α trivially). (round 3, imo-2026-03)
ALWAYS: when the global sort interleaves two classes (e.g. M-sub-pieces and R'-pieces), pair the merged list as (p_1,p_2),(p_3,p_4),… and classify each pair by origin — mixed pairs (odd-class-A, even-class-B) SELF-COMPENSATE by within-pair sortedness (r_odd ≥ m_even), reducing the inequality to a residual on same-origin pairs. This is the clean way to peel the interleaving obstruction. (round 3, imo-2026-03)

ALWAYS: when a dispatch asks to "lift a grid classification to reals," separate what the parity/grid theorem actually FORCES (a necessary condition on equality, derivable in one paragraph from the certified grid-parity lemma) from what is EMPIRICAL (the census's "achievable values" like `a = 2^j` — strategy-existence, not parity-forced). Prove the necessary condition cleanly and explicitly flag the empirical part as unproved — this is honest partial progress, not a fake lift (round 4, imo-2026-03 two-regime-disjunctive).
ALWAYS: for regime-N (non-dyadic upper-bound) sliver forcing, a clean BROADER class beats isolated configs — the balanced config `(w,...,w)` admits an explicit sliver strategy giving `A = 2s → 0` for ALL n (n odd: sliver-only; n even: bisect-one + slivers from n−1), proved by a sign-sum computation over the sorted blocks. Sign-sum over odd-length blocks starting at rank 2 is `−1`, over odd-length blocks starting after an even-length cancellation is `+1`; net `A = w − (w−s) + s = 2s`. Verified exact-rational n=1..7 (round 4, imo-2026-03).
NEVER: claim a sliver-forcing lemma closes regime-N universally — the balanced/all-n cover plus a few isolated n=3 configs (two-dyadic, extreme-dominant L>4/5) is partial; the moderately-dominant middle range and near-dyadic perturbations stay open. State the uncovered sub-classes explicitly (round 4, imo-2026-03).

ALWAYS: when a Hall-matching residual lives on the merged sort of a superincreasing geometric-sequence class and a bounded-sum class, derive the CLOSED FORM of A in terms of order statistics first (e.g. for level-3 unrefined-R, `A = 7 − 2(s_3+s_5)` in 1/15 units) — this linearizes the obstruction to ONE rank-index sum, turning the Hall matching into a clean casework on a single order statistic (t_2 = 2nd-largest) with three disjoint exhaustive cases (round 4, imo-2026-03 pairing-partner).
ALWAYS: for the unrefined-R sub-case of a self-similar dyadic decomposition (k = n+1, all marks in the dominant piece, R-pieces intact), the superincreasing structure `a_j − Σ_{l>j} a_l = α(n+1)` (the dyadic-dominance identity) IS the lever crux aimo-0019 names — each MM smaller half sits below a distinct dyadic level of R. Bound the obstruction magnitude by R_largest (Σ_MM m_even ≤ M − m_1 ≤ M/2 = a_1) as a structural corollary (round 4, imo-2026-03).
NEVER: expect the per-position rank dominance `s_{2j} ≤ a_{j+1}` to hold for the merged-sort Hall matching — it FAILS (counterexample b=(4/3,4/3,4/3) at n=2 gives s_4 = 4/3 > a_3 = 1). The matching is genuinely on the SUM over rank indices, subtler than per-position dominance. Also NEVER expect the layer-cake sufficient condition `⌊c(τ)/2⌋ ≤ c_A(τ)` to hold — it is too strong (same counterexample violates it at τ=1.3). The staircase interleaving (b's straddling R-levels) is the equality case, attained as a supremum (round 4, imo-2026-03).
NEVER: assume the superincreasing-R lever extends to R-REFINED sub-cases — when Xiang spends marks in R, the R'-pieces are fragments and the strict R-level structure breaks; the lever only adapts cleanly to the unrefined-R sub-case (k = n+1). The R-refined sub-cases (k ≤ n) need a different argument or a reduction showing unrefined-R is extremal (round 4, imo-2026-03).

ALWAYS: for a piecewise-linear alternating-sum bound on a mark-simplex, the vertex-principle (continuous + piecewise-linear on a compact polytope ⟹ min at an arrangement vertex) closes the bound at fixed n — BUT the arrangement must include PIECE-EQUALITY hyperplanes (two sub-pieces tie, where the sort swaps) and PIECE-ZERO hyperplanes (sub-piece=0, mark at Liu mark / coincidence / endpoint), NOT just the Liu-mark coordinate grid. The naive Liu-mark grid (`x_i ∈ {0,1/15,3/15,7/15,1}` for n=3) gives only the degenerate all-marks-at-Liu-marks vertices (all A=1/3 for imo-2026-03 n=3) and misses the real min (A=1/15, attained at the mirror 8/15,12/15,14/15, which is in a cell interior on a piece-equality facet). The continuity argument (a vanishing sub-piece contributes 0 to A regardless of parity, so A extends continuously across coincidence/Liu-mark boundaries) is what licenses the vertex reduction over reals. The "minimizer is a positive-dimensional flat facet" worry does NOT defeat the vertex check — the flat facet's vertices also attain the min, so the vertex check captures it; the flat-facet analysis is needed only to characterize EQUALITY, not the BOUND (round 4, imo-2026-03 cell-complex-l3, closed L(3) for reals via 11523-vertex exact-rational enumeration).

ALWAYS: when a certified lemma's COROLLARY is found false (the identity stands but a derived bound fails in a sub-case), split the proof on the corollary's hidden hypothesis rather than abandoning the lemma — for imo-2026-03 the false `σ ≤ M/2 = a_1` corollary held only under `m_1 ≥ M/2` (i.e. k=1); the fix was the m_1-split (Branch 1 `m_1 ≥ a_1` where the bound genuinely holds + the round-4 casework is valid; Branch 2 `m_1 < a_1` handled by a NEW casework). Remove the false corollary, keep the identity, propose the correction to the reviewer (round 5, pairing-partner).
NEVER: trust "largest of k+1 pieces summing to M is ≥ M/2" for k+1 ≥ 3 — the correct bound is `m_1 ≥ M/(k+1)`, which can be strictly below M/2. The `m_1 ≥ M/2` step only holds when there are exactly 2 pieces (k=1). Verify the averaging bound before citing it (round 5, found the superincreasing-R corollary bug).
ALWAYS: when fixing a proof that only covers half the configs (an unstated hypothesis like `m_1 ≥ a_1`), check the OTHER half's boundary behavior numerically — for imo-2026-03 Branch 2, the min A approaches α(n+1) in the limit `m_1 → a_1⁻` (boundary with Branch 1), confirming the bound is TIGHT at the boundary and no interior equality exists; this validates the two branches meet continuously (round 5, pairing-partner).
ALWAYS: when a dispatch flags a "cheap-kill" branch as OVERSTATED (outline-reviewer: "reduces to a Hall-type matching, NOT one-line"), believe the reviewer — derive the reduction explicitly and mark it as a GAP if no analytic proof exists. NEVER: treat a verified-conjecture-with-growing-margin as a proof — Branch 2 of imo-2026-03's m_1-split is verified n=1..5 with min A exceeding α by a growing margin, but the analytic Hall matching (H2) on the rest is still OPEN for general n. ALWAYS: for a small fixed n (e.g. n=2 = level-3 dyadic), a Hall-type matching GAP that's open for general n can often be CLOSED by direct casework — exhaust the order-statistic cases (which piece is rank-2, rank-3, etc.) with a disjoint split on the M-sub-piece order statistics vs the R-rest pieces. ALWAYS: when the cell-complex vertex-principle reduces a real bound to a finite vertex enumeration, the structural generalization (characterizing the winning vertex WITHOUT enumeration) splits cleanly into (a) integer-valued vertices where grid-parity (pair-excess mod 2) closes the bound in one line + gives the binary equality characterization, and (b) fractional-valued vertices which need a SEPARATE argument. Test whether fractional vertices are all strictly above the target — for imo-2026-03 n=3,4 the min A over fractional vertices is 5/3 (=1/9, 5/93) strictly > α(n), confining equality to integer vertices. NEVER: expect grid-parity on a SCALED fractional config to lift the real bound — scaling a fractional arrangement vertex by its denominator L gives integer pieces summing to L·D(n), but grid-parity needs L·D(n) ODD; if L is even the parity argument yields only A≥0, and even if L is odd it gives A ≥ 1/L which is WEAKER than α(n) when L > 1. The fractional-vertex bound requires a genuinely finer (rank-parity / dyadic-level) argument, not a parity-rescaling trick (round 5, imo-2026-03).
ALWAYS: when an inductive lift L(n)⟹L(n+1) uses a self-similar M⊎R decomposition, derive EXACTLY what the inductive input delivers before declaring the step — for imo-2026-03, L(n) applied to R gives o_R ≥ M/2 (NOT the needed e_M ≤ o_R), because the M-vs-R identity M−total(R)=α(n+1) minus the R-internal advantage A_R≥α(n+1) yields M≤2o_R. For imo-2026-03 the 4-mark shave became a 3-mark sliver "match b,c in d + shave a" giving A = |2d−1| = 1−2d < α in the d<1/2 gap — verify the mark COUNT stays within Xiang's n-mark budget (the 4-mark version was over budget; the 3-mark split-pair version stayed in budget) (round 5, imo-2026-03).

ALWAYS: when an inductive lift has a "factor-of-2 gap" (induction delivers only half the target), run exact-rational sampling of the FULL target inequality across n=2..6 — if 0 violations AND the slack `target_LHS − RHS` GROWS with n, the gap is a PROOF-TOOLING gap (the induction is too weak a tool, the inequality is true and loose), NOT a real obstruction. ALWAYS: sharpen a certified superincreasing identity `a_j − Σ_{l>j} a_l = α` to its GEOMETRIC form when the pieces are dyadic — `a_j = 2·a_{j+1}` (ratio 2, smallest term `= α`). The geometric-ratio-2 form is the actual lever for any Hall injection (the dyadic anchors), and the staircase equality config `{a_1,a_1,a_2,a_2,…,a_n,a_n,a_{n+1},a_{n+1},a_{n+1}}` (pair-pile) is provably admissible and attains `A = α` for ALL n via pair-cancellation + leftover — record this as a rigorous general-n equality theorem even when the matching itself is open (round 6, imo-2026-03 pairing-partner §E–§F).

NEVER: assume a "self-reproducing invariant" (crux `aimo-0262` template) gives a UNIVERSAL cap just because it reproduces on the equality locus — the pair-pile reproduces on the ridge (A=α, all n) and on the active+below perturbation (2-strategy family, A≤α), but OVERSHOOTS on "active grows from above" (both dist-1 and dist-2 fail) and on far-from-dyadic configs (mirror probed dead). ALWAYS: when adapting a crux (e.g. aimo-0262 Cinderella/Stepmother) to a different problem, separate the MECHANISM (self-reproducing invariant) from the BOUND TECHNIQUE (disjoint-pair averaging) — the disjoint-pair averaging of aimo-0262 (y_0+y_1+y_2+y_3≤2 ⟹ one pair≤1) does NOT translate when the target is a SUM (A=Σe_i) rather than a MAX of buckets; only the self-reproduction mechanism adapts, and it covers only the near-dyadic regime. Do not claim the bound technique transfers when it does not (round 6, imo-2026-03 self-reproducing-invariant §4 Gap 3).
NEVER: use integer division `sz/2` in Python 3 on a Fraction config — it returns a float (8/2=4.0), breaking exact-rational sum checks. Use `sz/F(2)` (Fraction division) throughout verification scripts. A subtle bug: `8 + F(16,7) + ...` mixes int and Fraction, and `8/F(2)` gives float 4.0, so `sum(result) == D(n)` can fail silently. Convert ALL config pieces to `Fraction` before any arithmetic (round 6, imo-2026-03 self_repro_verify.py).
