## imo-2026-03 — route: UNIQUE-EXTREMUM / SECOND-ORDER INSTABILITY (unifying variational framing)

**Conjecture (U-E) (dispatch):** the level-n dyadic Liu config is the unique global MAXIMIZER of `Φ(Liu) := min_{Xiang} A(Liu, Xiang)` (Xiang's best cap on the advantage sum), with `Φ(dyadic) = α(n) = 1/D(n)`. Equivalently `max_Liu Φ(Liu) = α(n)`, unique at the dyadic. (Note: the dispatch text says "minimizer of Φ" but the math is MAX — Liu maximizes her guaranteed `A`; the dyadic is where Xiang is WORST off, i.e. `Φ` is largest. I report on the max.)

### 1. Does (U-E) hold computationally for n=2, 3? — YES (n=2 a theorem; n=3 numerically)

**n=2 — THEOREM (already certified).** The `U(2)` four-strategy family (certified `lemmas/lemma-u2-four-strategy.md`) gives the EXACT closed form, in advantage coordinates:
> `Φ_2(a,b,c) = min( a, b−a, c−b, |2c−1| )`   for `a ≤ b ≤ c`, `a+b+c=1`  (infima, attained as sliver limits; Strategy A exact).

At the dyadic `(1/7, 2/7, 4/7)`: `a=1/7, b−a=1/7, c−b=2/7, |2c−1|=|8/7−1|=1/7`, so `Φ = 1/7 = α(2)`. The 4-way contradiction (certified) proves `min ≤ 1/7` with equality iff `(a,b,c)=(1/7,2/7,4/7)`. Together with `L(2)` (Xiang cannot push the dyadic below `1/7`, certified), this is **(U-E) for n=2 = `L(2) ∧ U(2)`, already a theorem** — just not previously stated in the variational "unique global max of Φ" language. (The coarse-grid scan on `N=84` that showed 36 configs tying at `1/7` is the known coarse-grid artifact — per-role rule: need `D≥2520`; the closed form supersedes it.)

**n=3 — numerically CONFIRMED (conjecture, not proof).**
- `Φ(dyadic_3)` = `1/15` to machine precision (`err = 2.8e-17`, scipy Nelder-Mead + differential-evolution with 20 restarts). The pair-pile gives `Φ ≤ 1/15` (certified, all n); numerically Xiang cannot push below — this is the open `L(3)`-for-reals, confirmed here.
- **Broad scan: 1200 random non-dyadic n=3 Liu configs.** `max Φ = 0.0595 < 1/15 = 0.0667`. **Zero configs reached `0.9·α(3)`**; 116/1200 exceeded `0.5·α(3)`. Distribution: `min≈0` (sliver floor, `A→0`), `50%=0.0134`, `95%=0.0394`, `max=0.0595`. The argmax of the random sample `(0.534, 0.593, 0.723)` is FAR from the dyadic `(0.067, 0.200, 0.467)` — a different basin, still below `α(3)`. Several non-dyadic configs hit the sliver floor `Φ≈0` (`A=0`, `Liu=1/2`): equal `(1/4,1/4,1/4,1/4)`, `(.5,.3,.15,.05)`, `(.5,.2,.2,.1)`, extreme `(.9,.033,.033,.033)` — `A=0` is the infimum (a zero leftover piece is excluded, but a sliver `→0` attains it in the limit). This is consistent with the (now-falsified for general n) `A≤0` pairing being the *infimum* for many non-dyadic configs even though the true cap is `>0` for some.
- **Conclusion: (U-E) holds for n=2 (theorem) and n=3 (numerically, robustly).** n=2's `Φ` closed form is the model: a `min` of finitely many piecewise-linear functionals, maximized uniquely at the dyadic where they all coincide.

### 2. Second-order / instability structure — STRICT LOCAL MAX with a downward KINK (first-order, not quadratic)

Probed the dyadic_3 neighborhood. **20 random directions at `eps=0.005`: ALL 20 give `Φ < 1/15` (DEC), zero increase, zero flat.** The dyadic is a **strict local maximum** of `Φ`.

**Scaling (one direction `d=(0.5,−0.4,0.3)/‖·‖`):** `eps` vs `delta = Φ−1/15`:
| eps | delta | delta/eps |
|---|---|---|
| 0.001 | −8.5e-4 | −0.85 |
| 0.002 | −1.7e-3 | −0.85 |
| 0.004 | −3.4e-3 | −0.85 |
| 0.008 | −1.58e-2 | −1.98 |
| 0.016 | −3.17e-2 | −1.98 |

`delta/eps` is piecewise-CONSTANT (−0.85 for tiny `eps`, switching to −1.98 around `eps≈0.006`). So `Φ` is **piecewise-LINEAR in the perturbation**, with a kink (non-differentiable point) at the dyadic. Near-dyadic perturbations `[t,−t/2,t/3]` confirm: `delta = −0.67·t` (small `t`) steepening to `−1.94·t` (larger `t`).

**Mechanism.** `Φ(Liu) = min_{Xiang} A(Liu, Xiang)`. For fixed Xiang marks, `A` is piecewise-linear in the Liu marks (sums of ±piece lengths, with the sign assignment depending on the sorted order). At the dyadic, MANY Xiang strategies are simultaneously active (the pair-pile and its tie-variants all give `A=1/15`); perturbing Liu breaks the tie, and `min` selects the strategy whose `A` decreases fastest. So `Φ` is a `min` of piecewise-linear pieces — a downward kink (concave-ish corner) at the dyadic. **This is first-order (linear) instability in every direction, not second-order (quadratic).** It is a "sharp" strict local max.

**Implication for (U-E) the dispatch asked about.** The dyadic is a strict LOCAL max via first-order non-improvement (the kink). For a SMOOTH function a strict local max need not be global; for a CONCAVE function it would be. `Φ = min` of piecewise-linear functions is *piecewise-linear* and locally concave-ish (min of affine is concave on each region where the active piece is fixed), but GLOBALLY `Φ` is only piecewise-concave — a strict local max of a piecewise-linear function is NOT automatically global. So the "if `Φ` is nice, global" step is the genuinely hard part (see §4).

### 3. Why this escapes both dead engines

- **G1's `e_M ≤ o_R` / pair-excess decomposition (pairing-partner, Engine C):** that route DECOMPOSES `A` into pair-excesses `(p_{2i−1}−p_{2i})` and chases the interleaving residual `(Match) Σ_MM m_even ≤ Σ_RR r_odd` on a fixed config. The `−2T` parity-flip wall (certified dead, `lemma-delta-a-local-cut`) lives in this decomposition — it is the tail-flip when a mark crosses a rank boundary. **(U-E) does not decompose `A` into pair-excesses at all.** It treats `Φ = min_Xiang A` as a single variational object indexed by Liu, and studies its first-order behavior at the extremum. The `−2T` term never appears because there is no per-mark local cut — the perturbation is in LIU-space (move Liu's marks), not in Xiang-space (add Xiang marks one at a time). The kink/first-order analysis sidesteps the interleaving obstruction entirely.
- **G2's regime/pile decomposition (two-regime-disjunctive, Engine R-pile):** that route CONSTRUCTS a greedy Xiang strategy (recursive pile-match of the two largest) per regime and proves `A < α(n)`. It is FALSIFIED (3 counterexample classes — greedy is suboptimal: it picks a bad cut, not an interleaving failure). **(U-E) does not construct ANY Xiang strategy.** It only needs to PROVE that for every non-dyadic Liu config, SOME Xiang strategy (existence, not construction) forces `A < α(n)` — equivalently, `Φ(Liu) < α(n)`. The variational proof would show this via the first-order conditions at the extremum + global structure, NOT by exhibiting the strategy. So (U-E) escapes greedy suboptimality by being non-constructive on the Xiang side.

### 4. The hard step — LOCAL is easy, GLOBAL is exactly G2

**(Local — easy, new partial lemma candidate):** "the dyadic is a strict local max of `Φ`" follows from the downward kink: at the dyadic, perturbing Liu breaks the multi-strategy tie and `min` selects a strictly-decreasing piece. This is a first-order non-improvement argument (cf. the crux below). Verified for n=3 (20/20 directions decrease, piecewise-linear scaling). A builder could package this as a partial lemma (n=2 fully via the closed form; n=3 numerically-supported, the analytic proof would characterize the active strategies at the dyadic and show each has a strictly-decreasing directional derivative in every direction).

**(Global — the hard step, and it IS G2):** showing no non-dyadic Liu config has `Φ ≥ α(n)` is *literally* the regime-N upper-bound conjecture. The variational framing does not SHORT-CIRCUIT G2; it RESTATES G2 as "the global max of `Φ` is at the dyadic." The local-to-global gap is exactly the open regime-N gap. A concavity/convexity argument on `Φ` would close it (local max of a concave function is global), but `Φ` is only PIECEWISE-concave (min of piecewise-linear), and the broad scan shows `Φ` is FAR below `α(n)` away from the dyadic (no near-misses: max over 1200 random configs is `0.0595`, `14%` below `α(3)=0.0667`) — so if a global argument exists it has slack, but no smooth-concavity certificate is available (`A` is neither Schur-convex nor Schur-concave, certified dead round 3).

**Partial result a builder could harvest:** (i) a NEW **n=2 unified-extremal lemma** — `Φ_2(Liu) = min(a, b−a, c−b, |2c−1|)`, `max = 1/7` unique at dyadic — packaging `L(2)∧U(2)` as one variational fact (content already certified, but the framing is new and reusable as the base case for an induction on `n` in `Φ`-space); (ii) a **n=3 local-strict-max lemma** (conjecture, numerically verified) — "the dyadic_3 is a strict local max of `Φ` with first-order (linear) decrease in every direction," which would be the first RIGOROUS local-instability result if the active-strategy characterization is proved. Neither closes G2.

### 5. Crux corpus — one genuinely analogous crux

Best match: **`aimo-0119`** (Dutch "cards in boxes" problem, combinatorics/extremal-principle). Crux move: *"Pick the configuration minimizing the maximum part load, tie-broken by fewest parts attaining that maximum, so that any single-item transfer from the heaviest to the lightest part is non-improving"*; then *"the non-improving swap gives `d_1 + d_100/11 ≥ d_100`,"* pinning the extremal config via first-order non-improvement. **This is the variational lever for (U-E):** pick the global extremum of `Φ`, impose that every local perturbation is non-improving, read off structure. The analogy is real (extremal-config + first-order-non-improvement → pin the config), but the global-to-local reduction in `aimo-0119` works because the objective is GLOBALLY concave (a max load is convex in the partition); here `Φ` is only piecewise-concave, so the same reduction stops at the local step.

Secondary: **`aimo-0117`** (dyadic-powers game). Crux: *"Assign the played values as a two-sided geometric (dyadic) sequence so that the single largest value strictly exceeds the sum of all the others."* Thematically adjacent (dyadic values, dominant-piece-exceeds-sum structure — the same `M − total(R) = α(n+1)` identity of `pairing-partner`), but the mechanism (maintain an invariant by deferring the extreme value) is different from variational extremization. Not a direct proof analogue.

No crux for "minimax saddle / symmetric config is the unique minimizer of a min-of-min" was found in the games-and-strategy or extremal-principle subtopics that truly resembles (U-E) beyond `aimo-0119`.

### 6. Honest assessment — genuinely different framing, but does NOT bypass the hard gap

**(U-E) is a genuinely DIFFERENT FRAMING** (variational / first-order-at-the-extremum, treating `Φ(Liu) = min_Xiang A` as one function of Liu) from both dead engines (G1 structural pair-excess decomposition; G2 greedy strategy construction per regime).
