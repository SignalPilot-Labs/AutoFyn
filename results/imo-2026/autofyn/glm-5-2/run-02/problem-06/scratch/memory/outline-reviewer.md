## Round 2 (imo-2026-06)
ALWAYS: when a "propagation via Bezout index-shift composition" route to Lemma 4 is filed, trace where the shift algebra comes from. The only natural index-shift algebra on P6's greedy is the residue-walk map φ on the transversal set V — but V is DEFINED via Lemma 4 (free-rider irrelevance), so any propagation proof that composes shifts through φ is CIRCULAR as a proof of Lemma 4. The aimo-0648 Bezout move composes shifts of a LINEAR recurrence; P6's greedy has no linear recurrence (the decision depends on the full prime history), so shifts do not compose in any pre-Lemma-4 sense. Either find a pre-Lemma-4 shift algebra (likely none exists) or label the route as speculative-with-circularity-risk. (because propagation-bezout was filed as a new route but the shift algebra needs Lemma 4, round 2)

ALWAYS: for any counting/double-counting route to "every pair shares a prime ≤ R", check whether the bound produced is the FIXED R or a GROWING (N−1)R window. Verified numerically: the large-prime-span lemma (p > (N−1)R divides ≤ 1 of the first N terms) is clean and provable, but it only yields "every pair shares a prime ≤ (N−1)R" (growing), NOT "≤ R" (fixed). Sharpening the threshold from (N−1)R to R requires the free-rider dichotomy = the crux itself. A counting route with a growing-window ceiling is a legitimate partial (the large-prime-span lemma is certifiable) but NOT a proof of Lemma 4 — do not let the builder claim closure. (because grid-counting-shared-primes was filed with the growing-window gap honestly flagged, round 2)

(because the crux-descent explorer surfaced it and it certified cleanly, round 2)
