## imo-2026-05

Field of 3 rival whole-problem approaches for IMO 2026 P5 (`determine all f:R_{>0}->R_{>0} with sqrt((x^2+f(y)^2)/2) >= (f(x)+y)/2 >= sqrt(x f(y))`). All three target the WHOLE claim end-to-end: existence (`f(x)=x+c, c>=0` via AM-GM/QM-AM on `(x,f(y))`) + uniqueness (`g=f-id` constant). All three share the proven preamble (`f(f(y))=2f(y)-y`, `g circ f = g`, `g>=0`, orbits are APs, `f` injective); they diverge in the uniqueness-closing FRAMING/ROUTE — kept far apart. Each is written to its approach file with the file-contract sections (`## Status = partial`, `## Approaches tried`, `## Current best`, proof-plan skeleton with hard steps marked as explicit **gaps**).

The tautology catalog (substitutions `x=y`, `y=f(x)`, iterate re-plugging, raw `x<->y` swap, middle=AM) is recorded in all three as dead ends — no step builds on any of them.

### gm-lipschitz-partition — recommend-new (but flag the gap honestly)
File: `results/imo-2026-05/approaches/gm-lipschitz-partition.md`. **Framing (what makes it distinct):** local, analytic / Lipschitz-flavored. The crux instrument is `|g(z)-g(y)| <= (sqrt(f(z))-sqrt(f(y)))^2` (Fact 5, derived from the RHS inequality at the swapped equality-forcing substitutions `x=f(z)` and `x=f(y)`), and uniqueness is closed by a discrete partition of a compact interval into `n` equal parts, summing the per-step quadratic self-bound `|Delta g| <= (Delta f)^2/(4a)` and sending `n->infty`. The LHS (RMS) inequality is shown redundant for uniqueness. **Single hardest gap (G1):** the quadratic self-bound `u_i <= (h+u_i)^2/(4a)` admits TWO branches — the small branch `u_i <= ~ h^2/(4a)` (sums to `O(1/n)->0`, giving constancy) AND a large-deviation branch `u_i >= ~ 4a` that Fact 5 alone does NOT rule out. The explorer's "verify `|Delta g|<=|Delta t|` inductively" is a hand-wave (no base case excluding the large branch); a wild step-function `g` with jumps of size `~4a` actually satisfies Fact 5 locally, so the partition via Fact 5 alone is INSUFFICIENT to force constancy of wild `g`. The builder must close G1 by (i) invoking local boundedness of `g` on compacts (from `(star star)`, Step 4 — straightforward) to localize to a sub-interval where the bound `M < u_+ ~ 4a` excludes the large branch, AND (ii) for points where `g(a) >= 4a`, invoking the ORIGINAL inequality `(star)` at near-jump pairs to forbid discontinuities — or importing the orbit-AP exclusion from `orbit-close-encounter`. This is the approach's true crux; it is the most elegant IF it closes but the riskiest to wild-`g` counterexamples. Recommend **new**; the reviewer should rank it high on the strength of Fact 5 (rigorously verified) but flag G1 as the gate.

### orbit-close-encounter — recommend-new (the most-complete route; likely the leader)
File: `results/imo-2026-05/approaches/orbit-close-encounter.md`. **Framing (what makes it distinct):** purely algebraic, global, orbit-AP + number-theoretic. Rewrites both inequalities in `g`-form to get two asymmetric two-point constraints `(star): 4 x g(y) <= (x-y)^2 + 2(x+y)g(x)+g(x)^2` and `(star star)` (the swap); within a level set both are tautological, so all content is across distinct level sets. Forces (A) `g` takes at most one positive value (two distinct positive values `c_a<c_b` on two unbounded forward APs close-encounter at large `t` within `c_a/2`; `(star)` at the close pair gives `4t(c_b-c_a) <= (5/4)c_a^2`, bounding `t` — contradiction), and (B) a fixed point forces `g equiv 0` (via the global quadratic upper bound `(dagger): g(y) <= (y-x_0)^2/(4x_0)` plus the universal lower bound `c >= (3+2sqrt2)x_0` on any positive step, which makes a `(3+2sqrt2)`-growing zero-region around each fixed point cover all of `R_{>0}` in finitely many multiplicative steps). Handles wild/non-measurable `g` directly (no continuity assumed). **Three hard gaps, each flagged:** G-A1 = the clean close-encounter number-theory lemma (Kronecker equidistribution for irrational step ratio + Bezout/periodic residue classes for rational coprime ratio; must state distinct residue classes do not collide); G-B1 = the `3+2sqrt2` straddle algebra (reconcile the straddle case giving `c>=16x_0` with the entirely-above case giving `c>=(3+2sqrt2)x_0`; take the min as the universal bound, verify the entirely-above quadratic `(c/x_0)^2-6(c/x_0)+1>=0` and its larger root); G-B2 = the interval-cover iteration (growth factor `3+2sqrt2>1` per step, `bigcup_k (0,(3+2sqrt2)^k x_0) = R_{>0}`). Also G-A2 = the orientation check (`c_b` must sit in the `g(y)` slot of `(star)`, not the reverse). This route is the most complete and the most robust to wild `g`. Recommend **new** and likely the leader; the reviewer should rank it highest IF the close-encounter lemma (G-A1) is accepted as standard.

### asymptotic-vanishing-coefficient — recommend-new (the highest-variance long shot; honest about the obstruction)
File: `results/imo-2026-05/approaches/asymptotic-vanishing-coefficient.md`. **Framing (what makes it distinct):** asymptotic / coefficient-vanishing (aimo-0234 port). Writes `g(x) = c + h(x)` with `h` a bounded error, expands `(star)` around `g equiv c` to get `(star-h): (x-y-c)^2 + h(x)[2(x+y+c)+h(x)] - 4 x h(y) >= 0`, and attempts to send a free variable to infinity so the `h`-coefficient `2 x h(x) - 4 x h(y)` is the leading surviving term and must vanish, pinning `h equiv 0`. **Single hardest gap (G1, load-bearing crux):** the constant-`c` residual `(x-y-c)^2` is non-negative and `~ x^2`-dominant at infinity, so it SWALLOWS the `h`-perturbation under the naive `x->infty` (the inequality is trivially satisfied for large `x`). This is the obstruction the crux-corpus explorer flagged but did NOT resolve. The skeleton offers the builder three candidate closures: (G-1a) difference two orbit-shifted instances of `(star-h)` to cancel the `n^2 (c+h(s))^2` leading terms and leave an `n`-linear residual in `h(s)-h(t)` (most faithful to aimo-0234, but may reduce to a tautology like the iterate-equality point `x=f(y)` does — verified to give `0>=0`); (G-1b) evaluate at `x=f(y)` — verified TAUTOLOGICAL, do not pursue; (G-1c) reframe to extremal-value / supremum self-amplification (aimo-0787 port) — let `M=sup h`, derive `M <= M - (positive)` at the approximating-maximizer sequence. There is a prerequisite gap G-0: boundedness of `g` on a RAY (not just compacts) is needed for the limit and is NOT automatic — the compact bound `M([a,b]) ~ b^2/(4a)` grows with `b`; the builder must either prove a ray bound from the RMS side, or restrict to the extremal-value reframing (c) which only needs `sup h` finite. **Recommendation:** this is the riskiest framing — IF the gap G1 closes (via (a) or (c)) it is the most elegant and most distinct from the other two; if G1 proves fatal after the builder's attempt, the approach should be REVISED into `extremal-self-amplification` (the aimo-0787 reframing, a new slug), NOT abandoned. Recommend **new** but rank lowest of the three pending G-0/G-1 closure; the reviewer should flag it for likely revision.
