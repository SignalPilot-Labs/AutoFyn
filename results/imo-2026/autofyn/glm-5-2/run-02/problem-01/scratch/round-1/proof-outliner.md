## imo-2026-01

Field: 2 rival whole-attempt slugs (both target the FULL claim, parts (a)+(b)). The three
explorers independently converged on one solid core — the move is a per-prime
subtractive-Euclidean step on exponent pairs, giving (i) the invariant g_p =
gcd of all v_p-exponents (preserved by gcd(a,b)=gcd(min,|a−b|)), hence
M = ∏ p^{g_p}, and (ii) the lexicographic monovariant (W,C) = (ΣΩ, #{>1})
strictly decreasing every move. The two approaches below are genuinely different
ROUTINGS of this core, not technique-only variants: one proves the
invariant/uniqueness half first and attaches termination as a second half; the
other proves termination-and-at-most-one first and brings the invariant in only
to upgrade "≤1" to "exactly one" and pin M.

A genuinely-different THIRD framing was investigated and rejected: the
"gcd-lattice / divisibility-submonoid generated by the board" is NOT preserved
by the move (verified: {4,8}→{4,2} shrinks the lattice; {6,10}→{2,15} too).
Magnitude-based single-integer weights also fail (coprime move {6,35}→{1,210}
increases the sum and the max). The per-prime exponent decomposition is
essential — no viable number-level alternative invariant exists, since
"gcd of the numbers" = ∏ p^{min exponents} ≠ ∏ p^{gcd exponents} = Q.
So the honest field is 2 approaches.

Dead-ends recorded in BOTH approach files (do not retry):
- "gcd of all board numbers" NOT invariant ({4,8}: 4→{4,2}: 2).
- "min of p-exponents" (= v_p of gcd of numbers) NOT invariant ({4,8}: 2→1).
- "prime-power level-set count #{i: p^k|a_i}" NOT invariant ({4,8}, k=2: 2→1).
- "gcd-lattice generated by the board" NOT preserved (computed).
- "product of all entries" as SOLE termination argument fails (coprime moves
  leave it unchanged: {6,35}→{1,210}); only a sanity invariant for shared-prime.

invariant-first: new
Target: IMO 2026 P1 full claim — (a) process terminates with exactly one M>1; (b) M is choice-independent.
Technique: Invariant-first routing — per-prime exponent invariant g_p = gcd of all v_p (Euclidean-step preservation) pins terminal M = ∏ p^{g_p} and gives M≥2 BEFORE termination is proved; lexicographic monovariant (W,C) attached as second half to force a terminal state; bridge = termination⇒≤1 entry>1, invariant M≥2 forbids 0 ⇒ exactly one.
Skeleton:
  1. Reduce move to per-prime exponent step (a,b)→(min(a,b),|a−b|) — by v_p(gcd)=min, v_p(lcm/gcd)=max−min.
  2. Define g_p = gcd of all 2026 v_p-exponents (gcd(0,k)=k convention) — GAP: well-definedness + zero convention.
  3. Prove g_p invariant — by Euclidean identity gcd(a,b)=gcd(min(a,b),|a−b|) (subtractive form); GAP: (a,0)→(0,a) sub-case.
  4. Define Q = ∏_p p^{g_p} (finite product) — board invariant by step 3.
  5. Q ≥ 2 — some entry >1 ⇒ some prime divides an entry ⇒ g_p≥1; GAP: finiteness + lower bound.
  6. Terminal state has ≤1 entry >1 — because a move is legal iff two entries >1 exist; GAP: confirm lcm/gcd is always a valid integer, no other obstruction.
  7. Attach termination: (W,C) lex-decreases every move — casework {coprime, equal, intermediate}; GAP: full ΔW=−Ω(g) derivation + three-case table.
  8. Combine for (a): terminal reached (7) with ≤1>1 (6); all-1s would force Q=1 contradicting Q≥2 (5) ⇒ exactly one.
  9. Pin M = Q for (b): terminal valuation multiset {v_p(M),0,…,0} has gcd v_p(M) = g_p ⇒ M = Q.
Key lemmas (claim + mechanism):
  - g_p invariant — subtractive-Euclidean step on exponent pair preserves pair-gcd (Euclidean identity), zeros neutral via gcd(0,k)=k.
  - (W,C) strictly lex-decreases — ΔW=−Ω(gcd(m,n))≤0 (equality iff coprime), coprime moves drop C by 1; three cases {coprime,equal,intermediate} exhaust m,n>1.
  - terminal ⟺ ≤1 entry >1 — move legal iff two >1 entries exist.
  - M=Q in terminal — lone M's valuations are the board's valuation multiset; its gcd is g_p by invariance.
Open gaps: steps 2/3 (rigorous invariant + zero convention), 5 (Q≥2), 6 (terminal characterization), 7 (full monovariant casework), 8 (contradiction chain).
Cases to cover: three move cases {coprime, equal, intermediate} for (W,C); the equal case m=n is distinct (lcm/gcd=1).
Watch out for: conflating "gcd of exponents" with "min of exponents" (they differ on {4,8}: 1 vs 2); omitting the "not zero" half of (a); forgetting the m=n case.

monovariant-first: new
Target: IMO 2026 P1 full claim — (a) process terminates with exactly one M>1; (b) M is choice-independent.
Technique: Monovariant-first routing — lexicographic monovariant (W,C)=(ΣΩ,#{>1}) strictly decreasing every move proves termination and gives terminal ≤1 entry >1 FIRST; per-prime exponent invariant g_p brought in SECOND only to rule out all-1s (upgrade ≤1 to exactly one) and pin M=∏p^{g_p}.
Skeleton:
  1. Define W=ΣΩ(a_i), C=#{a_i>1} — nonneg integers, bounded.
  2. Reduce a move to (g, ab) form with m=ga, n=gb, gcd(a,b)=1 — by lcm·gcd=m·n.
  3. Casework ΔW, ΔC: coprime(g=1): ΔW=0,ΔC=−1; equal(m=n): ΔW=−Ω(m),ΔC=−1; intermediate(g>1,a,b>1): ΔW=−Ω(g),ΔC=0 — by Ω(ab)=Ω(a)+Ω(b) on coprime factors; GAP: exhaustiveness, Ω-additivity justification.
  4. (W,C) strictly lex-decreases every move ⇒ finitely many moves — by Invariants & monovariants + integer well-foundedness. [part (a) "finitely many"]
  5. Terminal state has ≤1 entry >1 — move legal iff two >1 entries; GAP: no other obstruction.
  6. Bring in g_p invariant — subtractive-Euclidean step on exponent pair, gcd(a,b)=gcd(min,|a−b|); GAP: full proof incl. (a,0) case + gcd(0,k)=k (or import certified lemma).
  7. Q=∏p^{g_p} board invariant, Q≥2 — finite product, some g_p≥1; GAP: finiteness + lower bound.
  8. All-1s terminal ⇒ Q=1 contradicting Q≥2 ⇒ exactly one entry >1. [completes (a)]
  9. Terminal valuation multiset {v_p(M),0,…,0} ⇒ v_p(M)=g_p ⇒ M=Q, choice-independent. [part (b)]
Key lemmas (claim + mechanism):
  - (W,C) strictly lex-decreases — ΔW=−Ω(gcd(m,n))≤0 with equality iff coprime, coprime moves drop C by 1.
  - g_p invariant — subtractive-Euclidean step preserves pair-gcd; zeros neutral.
  - terminal ⟺ ≤1 entry >1 — move legal iff two >1 entries.
  - M=Q in terminal — lone M's valuations are the board's valuation multiset; gcd is g_p.
Open gaps: steps 3 (full case table + Ω-additivity), 6 (invariant, or lemma import), 7 (Q≥2), 8 (contradiction chain).
Cases to cover: three move cases {coprime, equal, intermediate}.
Watch out for: the equal case m=n must be separate from intermediate (its C-account is −1, lcm/gcd=1); ΔW=−Ω(g) needs the m=ga,n=gb factorization and Ω-additivity on coprime a,b; termination alone gives only ≤1, the invariant Q≥2 is REQUIRED for "exactly one"; g_p=gcd of exponents ≠ min of exponents.
