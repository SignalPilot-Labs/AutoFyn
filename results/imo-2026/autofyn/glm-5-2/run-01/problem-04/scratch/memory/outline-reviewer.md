# outline-reviewer per-role rules

ALWAYS: test a conjectured monovariant by simulating the proposed greedy operation before approving a monovariant-based approach — a cycle under greedy play is strong evidence no strict monovariant exists for that op, and the winning play often turns out to be an already-known induction (round 1, imo-2026-04 chip-transfer).

NEVER: register an approach whose necessity AND sufficiency engines are both verbatim shared with another registered approach and whose only distinctive content is a reformulation/punchline that the outliner itself admits does not by itself prove the key step — that is a single-gap-trap near-duplicate that will die with its twin (round 1, imo-2026-04 modular-residue-orbit, refused).
