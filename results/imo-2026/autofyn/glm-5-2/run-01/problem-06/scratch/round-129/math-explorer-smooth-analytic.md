## imo-2026-06  (smooth-analytic / smooth-number-density lens)

Scouting the registered-but-never-built `smooth-window-crash` approach: can smooth-/small-prime number density prove that in every window `(a_n, a_n + mtp(M_n)]` there is a valid number divisible by some prime ≤ p* := min P(a_1), and does that close the saturated-regime wall (GAP-S)? Probes: `/tmp/round-129/smooth_probe.py`, `witness_probe.py`, `strict_beat_probe.py`. ~50 saturated seeds (odd a_1, ≥2 distinct prime factors, NOT freezing): 15, 35, 105, 165, 385, 429, 1001, 2145, 4199, 7429, 12673, 175, 187, 221, 323, 899, 1147, 1517, 1763, 2021, 2461, 31213, 667, 1189, 1207, 1387, 1591, 1739, 2501, 2773, 3059, 3239, 3713, 4331, 5293, 6499, 7387, 8633, 15341, 19549, 5183, 6161, 10403, 10787.

### Distinct openings (each a different attack the outliner could build)

1. **The "witness always carries a small prime" structural lemma (strongest find; makes window-statement (b) trivial).** Across **26 saturated seeds, at every computable step (~1800 step-records total), the mtp-witness transversal T\* ALWAYS contains a prime ≤ p* — 0 violations** (`witness_probe.py`). Consequence: the unique multiple of mtp in `(a_n, a_n+mtp]` (which is valid by the certified gap bound) is automatically divisible by a prime ≤ p*. So statement (b) — "there is a ≤p*-divisible valid number in `(a_n, a_n+mtp]`" — **holds trivially whenever T\* has a small prime; no Dickman/de Bruijn density needed.** This collapses the smooth-density question into a single cleaner structural claim about T\*, and discharges the "correlation problem" for (b): there is no correlation to fight because the mtp-multiple is a single guaranteed valid+small-prime point.

2. **Correction to the round-3 linchpin premise (important for the outliner).** The dispatch's premise "at a promotion a_{n+1} is NOT the mtp-multiple (else it would be dominated and not a new minimal)" is **REFUTED by data**: across 344 promotions, only **174 are strict beats** (a_{n+1} < mtp-multiple); the other **170 have a_{n+1} = mtp-multiple** (gap = mtp exactly) and are STILL promotions (new minimals enter). Reason: the mtp-witness T\* is a *transversal* of M_n, NOT generally a *member* of M_n, so `P(a_{n+1}) ⊇ T*` does NOT make `P(a_{n+1})` dominated (domination needs a member of M_n as subset). So the "strictly-smaller" mechanism is only half the story; for the 170 equality-promotions the small-prime property of a_{n+1} is inherited for free from T\* (since T\* ⊆ P(a_{n+1}) and T\* has a small prime — opening 1). **Only the 174 strict-beat promotions are the genuinely hard case for the smooth route.**

3. **The hard smooth step (strict-beat case): the SMALLEST valid number below the mtp-multiple carries a small prime.** In the 174 strict-beat promotions, a_{n+1} is a valid number strictly below the (small-prime-divisible) mtp-multiple. Data: a_{n+1} carries a prime ≤ p* in 100% of these too (0 violations of (a) across all ~3500 step-records). But this is NOT implied by opening 1 — the smallest valid below the mtp-multiple could in principle be a large-prime-only number. Proving it is a genuine **smooth-number-density fact**: small-prime-divisible integers are denser (multiples of 2 have density 1/2, of 3 density 1/3, …) than p\*-rough integers, so the smallest valid integer above a_n is overwhelmingly likely to be small-prime-divisible. **This is the one place a smooth-number theorem (Dickman ρ-function upper bound on p\*-rough numbers, or a direct sieve bound) is genuinely wanted.** No proof found this round; the data is a 0-violation CONJECTURE.

4. **Reduction of the smooth route to SPT, and the honest verdict that SPT does NOT close the wall.** "Every promotion's a_{n+1} carries a prime ≤ p*" ⟺ "every entering minimal M'=P(a_{n+1}) has min(M') ≤ p*" ⟺ **SPT** (the round-3 finite-state conjecture, 280 seeds 0 violations), since P(a_1) already satisfies it. So the smooth route's full target IS SPT. **BUT SPT alone does NOT close GAP-S** (contrary to the dispatch's framing): SPT only bounds `mtp(M_n) ≤ ∏{p ≤ p*}` (closes γ's GAP-1) — it does NOT bound P_ess, because a minimal like {2, 97} satisfies SPT (2 ≤ p*) yet 97 ∈ P_ess. A pairwise-intersecting antichain all containing some small prime from a bounded set can still be infinite ({q, r_i} for unbounded r_i). So **even a fully-proven smooth route closes GAP-1, not the wall**; the wall (GAP-3 / bounded P_ess) needs the *free-rider / bounded-entering-prime* mechanism (round-3 smallest-first lens), which the smooth-density argument does not supply. The smooth route is a genuinely-different *mechanism* (density of smooth numbers vs smallest-first free-rider dynamics) but it lands on a *different sub-gap* (GAP-1) than the one the wall actually needs (GAP-3).

5. **"2 in the witness" is FALSE; the witness small prime varies (a1=175 refutes the 2-anchor).** 291/344 promotions have 2 ∈ T\*, but a1=175=5²·7 has 2 in T\* only 2/81 steps (its witness small prime is 3 or 5). So no single small prime is the permanent anchor; the correct statement is "T\* contains SOME prime ≤ p\*" (opening 1), not "2 ∈ T\*". This kills any proof attempt relying on a permanent {2, p\*} transversal.

### Candidate technique(s)
- **Smooth-number density / sieve bounds** (Dickman ρ, Brun sieve, or elementary: count of p\*-rough numbers in a length-mtp interval is small) — wanted ONLY for the strict-beat hard step (opening 3). Standard analytic-number-theory machinery; NOT in `knowledge_base.md` (would need to be added or cited as an external standard result).
- **Invariants & monovariants** (KB): the certified `mtp-monovariant-and-gap-bound` supplies `a_{n+1}-a_n ≤ mtp` and monotonicity — the window `(a_n, a_n+mtp]` and the "mtp-multiple is valid" fact both come from here.
- **Pigeonhole/extremal** (KB): opening 1 (witness always has a small prime) is an extremal statement on transversals; a direct combinatorial proof (not density) may exist and would bypass smooth-number theory entirely for half the promotions.
- NOTE: the KB has **no Dickman/de Bruijn / smooth-number entry**; the outliner must either cite an external theorem (and prove the adaptation from scratch per repo rules) or find an elementary substitute. This is a real gap in the available toolkit.

### Cheap-kill candidates
- **Prove "T\* always contains a prime ≤ p\*" directly (no density).** If every M ∈ M_n has min(M) ≤ p\* (SPT), then S := Pess ∩ {primes ≤ p\*} is a transversal with product ≤ R := ∏{p ≤ p\*}; so mtp ≤ R. If T\* avoided all small primes, every prime in T\* is > p\*, and (saturated regime: no common prime, so |T\*| ≥ 2) prod(T\*) ≥ (nextprime(p\*))·(nextprime(p\*)+?) — not automatically > R. So a direct inequality `prod(large-only transversal) > R` does NOT hold in general (constructible counterfamilies exist). So the cheap structural kill is NOT obviously available; the witness-small-prime fact is genuinely empirical-and-tight, not a formality. Worth one focused proof attempt but do not assume.
- **mtp ≤ a_1** (round-3 finite-state CONJECTURE, 138 seeds) — if provable it closes GAP-1 directly without SPT; the smooth route is then redundant for GAP-1. The outliner should prefer whichever (SPT or mtp≤a_1) has a cleaner proof attack.
- Parity / pure pigeonhole / v_p / injection: none gave a one-move kill in probing (family is pairwise-intersecting, LLL vacuous — already known).

### Verdict on the smooth-window-crash framing

**PARTIALLY ALIVE — a genuinely-different mechanism, but it closes GAP-1 not the wall.** The smooth route supplies a real, different angle (small-prime density of the greedy's pick) and a strong 0-violation conjecture (T\* always has a small prime → (b) trivial → a_{n+1} carries small prime → SPT). BUT two honest caveats the outliner must weigh:

1. **Scope:** SPT (the smooth route's target) closes **GAP-1 (mtp bounded)**, NOT the wall (GAP-S / GAP-3). P_ess can still be unbounded under SPT. So the smooth route, even fully proved, is a **partial** wall-closer; it must be paired with the free-rider / bounded-entering-prime route (round-3 smallest-first lens) to close GAP-3. It is NOT a standalone rival solution to the whole wall.

2. **Hard step genuinely hard:** the strict-beat case (174 promotions) needs a *short-interval smooth-number density* bound — "the smallest valid in (a_n, mtp-multiple) is small-prime-divisible" — which is an analytic-NT statement not in `knowledge_base.md`, and standard Dickman bounds are asymptotic (x→∞) whereas the saturated regime keeps a_n ~ O(a_1) (so x is bounded-ish, where asymptotic density is meaningless). The elementary alternative (direct sieve / counting) is unproven here.

**Cleanest skeleton for the outliner (if it builds `smooth-window-crash`):**
- Hard step: prove "for every n in the saturated regime, the smallest valid integer > a_n carries a prime ≤ p\*." Split into (i) equality-case (a_{n+1} = mtp-multiple): trivial GIVEN lemma "T\* contains a prime ≤ p\*"; (ii) strict-beat case: genuine smooth-density step, the wall of this approach.
- Supporting lemma to propose for certification: **"T\* contains a prime ≤ p\*"** (0 violations, 26 seeds) — a direct combinatorial proof may exist; if not, it folds into SPT itself.
- Theorem to invoke: a short-interval smooth/rough-number bound (Dickman ρ or elementary sieve) — must be cited externally and re-proved/adapted per repo rules; KB has no such entry.
- Gap: (a) no proof of the strict-beat smooth-density step; (b) even with SPT proved, GAP-3 remains — the smooth route does not stand alone.
- Recommendation: build `smooth-window-crash` ONLY as a GAP-1-closing approach paired with a GAP-3 approach (free-rider), NOT as a standalone whole-wall rival; and prioritize the direct combinatorial proof of "T\* has a small prime" over heavy analytic density machinery, since the data shows the small prime is always present structurally, not just asymptotically.
