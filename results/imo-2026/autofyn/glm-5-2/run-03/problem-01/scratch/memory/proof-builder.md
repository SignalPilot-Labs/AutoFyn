# proof-builder role rules

ALWAYS: re-derive every load-bearing algebraic identity from per-prime exponents when the move couples primes (a wrong parenthetical like "2Ω(gcd)" slips past additivity; the per-prime sum min(α,β) is the unambiguous source of truth), round 1.
ALWAYS: write a promotable shared lemma to results/<id>/lemmas/ when the approach proves a step (invariant or monovariant) that a sibling approach reuses — the confluence-newman route needs the (Ω,K) monovariant and d_p invariant it does not re-prove, round 1.
NEVER: trust the outliner's parenthetical arithmetic — the reviewer catches slip errors in those; recompute the identity yourself before writing it into the proof, round 1.

NEVER: assume per-prime local confluence lifts to the whole-number system when moves couple primes — for the gcd/lcm blackboard (imo-2026-01), the per-prime 1D Euclidean rewrite IS locally confluent, but the joining sequences per prime need not synchronize into one valid whole-number move sequence; the symmetric 2-step completion of the overlapping critical pair {a,b,c} fails ~97% of the time, joins run as deep as 3+3 moves with no fixed pattern, so Newman's-lemma framing of (b) is a genuine open gap, not a hand-wave target, round 1.
