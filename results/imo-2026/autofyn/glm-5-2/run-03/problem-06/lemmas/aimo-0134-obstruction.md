# Lemma (negative): the `aimo-0134` integer-monovariant mechanism does not port

**Statement (negative).** The `aimo-0134` eventual-constancy-via-monovariant mechanism — define an integer-valued running statistic, use integrality to upgrade a strict inequality to weak monotonicity, conclude eventual constancy, transfer back to the original sequence — does **not** port to the greedy-gcd setting of `imo-2026-06`. Concretely, for the two natural analogs of the `aimo-0134` auxiliary $b_k$:

(i) The cumulative defect $C_n:=nM_1-(a_{n+1}-a_1)=\sum_{k=1}^{n}(M_1-d_k)$ is non-decreasing (forced by the certified gap bound $d_k\le M_1$) but **unbounded in every non-LOCK case** (a bounded non-negative integer series $\sum(M_1-d_k)$ would force $M_1-d_n=0$ for all sufficiently large $n$, i.e. $d_n\equiv M_1$ eventually, i.e. $a_{n+1}-a_n\equiv M_1$ — a period-$1$ AP with $L=M_1$, contradicting non-LOCK where $L/T<M_1$). Hence eventual constancy of $C_n$ cannot be concluded in non-LOCK cases.

(ii) The running-average floor $b_n^{\rm avg}:=\left\lfloor\frac{a_n-a_1}{n-1}\right\rfloor$ ($n\ge2$) is bounded ($\le M_1$) and eventually constant (value $\lfloor L/T\rfloor$) *whenever* $(d_n)$ is periodic (Cesàro), but is **non-monotone** (neither non-decreasing nor non-increasing) in every non-LOCK case — verified concretely for $a_1=15$: $b_2^{\rm avg}=3>b_3^{\rm avg}=2<b_4^{\rm avg}=3$, and for $a_1\in\{35,77,91,175,847\}$. Hence the bounded-monotone-integer $\Rightarrow$ eventual-constancy lemma does not apply.

**Root cause (rigorous).** The `aimo-0134` monotonicity step requires a *shrinking* range bound: the bound on $a_{k+1}$ is $k$, small relative to $k+1$, producing $b_{k+1}<b_k+1$ with slack that integrality upgrades to $b_{k+1}\le b_k$. In the greedy-gcd setting the only available bound on the increment is the **constant** gap bound $d_n\le M_1$ (certified). A constant bound produces only the tautological $b_{n+1}^{\rm avg}\le b_n^{\rm avg}+1$, with no slack for integrality to exploit. The greedy "smallest admissible" rule gives minimality of $d_n$ (an equality), not an inequality against a running average. No shrinking bound is available to drive the integrality upgrade.

**Transfer step is also Gap A.** Even granting a counterfactual eventually-constant statistic, the finite-state pigeonhole transfer to $d_n$-periodicity on the state $(c_n,\text{recent }d\text{-window})$ is not closed: the transition depends on the free-rider primes of $S(a_{n+1})$, not on the state (89 conflicts for $a_1=385$ on $a_n\bmod M_1$; round-3 finite-statistic explorer). Closing the state = bounding the free-rider governing primes = **Gap A**.

**Consequence.** Future outliners should NOT re-dispatch the `aimo-0134` integer-monovariant framing on this problem. A *different* integer-monovariant mechanism (not based on integrality-upgrading-a-strict-inequality from a shrinking range bound) is not ruled out by this lemma — but the `aimo-0134` template specifically is dead.

**Status.** Reviewer-certified (round 3) as a **negative lemma**. The contrapositive proof of (i) (bounded $\Rightarrow$ $d_n\to M_1$ $\Rightarrow$ not non-LOCK) is valid; the counterexample for (ii) is re-verified ($a_1=15$: $b^{\rm avg}=(3,2,3,\dots)$). Note: the builder's parenthetical "for LOCK cases $C_n\equiv0$" is **incorrect** — for LOCK cases with lock prime $p\ne M_1$ (e.g. $a_1=116$, $M_1=58$, $p=2$), $C_n$ grows linearly too; $C_n\equiv0$ holds only for the special LOCK case $a_1=p$ (single prime, $p=M_1$). This side-remark error does not affect the load-bearing claim (non-LOCK $\Rightarrow$ $C_n$ unbounded), which is proved by the valid contrapositive.
