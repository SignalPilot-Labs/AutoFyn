# Lemma (negative, structural): deviation-descent blocked by $w_{\min}$-fence (and cofactor fence)

**Statement (negative).** Consider the deviation-index descent route for imo-2026-06: mount a minimal-counterexample descent on the first index $n_0$ at which the increment-word $d$ deviates from a pigeonhole candidate period $P_0$ (supplied by `candidate-period-pigeonhole-existence`), attempting to produce a deviation at some $m<n_0$ and collapse by well-ordering. This descent **cannot work as a uniform mechanism in the window-length $w$**, and the only greedy-dynamic ingredient available to power it (the forced-increment identity $d_{m-1}=q-(a_{m-1}\bmod q)$ at $q$-multiple steps, from the certified Steps 1–6 of `minimal-criminal-schur-contradiction`) does not rescue it. Concretely:

1. **(Non-uniformity in $w$, computationally verified.)** For small $w$ the pigeonhole offset $P_0(w)$ is NOT a period of $d$, and a real deviation exists — so a uniform descent would falsely conclude periodicity. Witness: $a_1=35$, $M_1=35$, true $T=34$. With $w=2$: a valid length-$2$ coincidence occurs at $i=5$, $j=10$ ($P_0=5$), but $d_8=4\ne10=d_{13}$ — a real deviation at $n_0=8$. With $w=8$: the coincidence is $i=0$, $j=34$, $P_0=34=T$, no deviation. Hence the descent's conclusion ("no deviation $\Rightarrow$ $d$ is $P_0$-periodic") is FALSE for $w=2$ and TRUE for $w=8$; the mechanism cannot be uniform in $w$.

2. **(Reduction to the increment-window-automaton fence.)** For the descent to have a chance, $w$ must satisfy $w\ge w_{\min}$, where $w_{\min}$ is the minimal window-length recovering the true period. But $w_{\min}$ is UNBOUNDED in $M_1$ (the round-4 `ergodic-window-state / increment-window-automaton` fence: $w_{\min}>24$ for $a_1=847$, $M_1=77$; $w_{\min}\approx100$ for $a_1=385$, state size $\approx T=5088$). Bounding $w_{\min}$ a priori IS Gap A.

3. **(Cofactor-bound fence, independent.)** Even at a fixed $w\ge w_{\min}$, the descent's shift-by-$C$ argument ($C:=a_{j_0}-a_{i_0}$, the constant offset on the coincidence range $[i_0,n_0]$ proved by telescoping) does NOT preserve the admissibility structure: $\gcd(a_{n_0+1}+C, a_{j-P_0}+C)$ has no forced relation to $\gcd(a_{n_0+1}, a_{j-P_0})$ — adding $C$ to both arguments of a gcd does not preserve it. For the shift to preserve admissibility globally, $C$ must be divisible by every governing prime (a cofactor-type bound), fenced by `window-uniqueness-reduces-to-cofactor`, `lemma-C-strip-no-go`, and `schur-cofactor-premise-fails-in-periodic-regime`.

4. **(The forced-increment identity does not rescue.)** The mount's only greedy-DYNAMIC ingredient is the forced-increment identity $d_{m-1}=q-(a_{m-1}\bmod q)$ at $q$-multiple steps (where $q$ is the smallest governing prime $>M_1$, IF Gap A fails). It determines $d$ FORWARD and LOCALLY at $q$-multiple steps; the descent requires BACKWARD and GLOBAL propagation of a deviation. The deviation index $n_0$ is set by the $d$-word coincidence structure, not by the $q$-multiple pattern, so we cannot ensure $a_{n_0+1}$ is a $q$-multiple; and even if it were, the deviation becomes the statement "$q\nmid C$" — a statement about $C$ and $q$, not a smaller-index deviation.

**Consequence.** The deviation-descent route is blocked by TWO independent fences (the increment-window-automaton fence via $w_{\min}$, and the cofactor-bound fence via the shift-by-$C$). It does NOT close Gap A. Future deviation-descent retries must first break ONE of these two fences; absent that, the framing is dead.
