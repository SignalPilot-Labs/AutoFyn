## transversal-saturation: advance
Target: the whole theorem — there exist $T,L$ with $a_{n+T}=a_n+L$ for all $n\ge1$.
Technique: minimal-criminal + prime-factor stripping (port of crux `aimo-0030`, "size-bounding-and-descent" / "divisibility-and-gcd") to close Gap A; certified Lemmas 1–6, 9 carry the endgame.
Skeleton:
  1.   2. **Correct Step 7's factual errors.** Retract the false " $a_1=385$ aperiodic through 12000 terms" and " $q\le M_1$ refuted" narrative. State: $a_1=385$ IS periodic from $n=1$ ($L=43890=2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot7\cdot11\cdot19$, $T=5088$); its governing primes $\{2,3,5,7,11,19\}$ are all $\le M_1=385$. Crucially distinguish GOVERNING primes (factors of $L$, members of $\operatorname{MT}(\mathcal F_\infty)$) from TRANSIENT primes (in $\operatorname{MT}(\mathcal F_n)$ at finite $n$ but dropped by the limit — $a_1=145$ has transient primes up to 97; $a_1=2085$ has term-primes up to 2621). The bound $q\le M_1$ is on the GOVERNING set only; it holds in 273+ tested cases.
  3. **Gap A via the `aimo-0030` minimal-criminal strip.**
     a. Suppose $q>M_1$ is the smallest governing prime exceeding $M_1$, with a private witness $a_i$ ($S(a_i)\cap T=\{q\}$ for some $T\in\operatorname{MT}(\mathcal F_\infty)\ni q$).
     b. By linchpin, $a_i$ carries some $p\in P_1\cap S(a_i)$; since $p\le M_1<q$, $p\in S(a_i)\setminus T$ — the witness has a small $P_1$-prime outside $T$.
     c. Let $A=\prod_{r\mid a_i,\,r\le M_1}r$ (the small radical of $a_i$), pick $p\in P_1\cap S(a_i)$, and let $k$ be the least integer $\ge0$ with $x=p^k\cdot A>a_{i-1}$.
     d. **Strip size bound (`aimo-0030` product comparison):** $x\le a_i$. Mechanism: $a_i=q\cdot A'$ with $A'=a_i/q$; $A\mid A'$ (the small radical divides the small part of $a_i$); replacing the large factor $q>M_1\ge\prod_{\text{small }r\mid a_i}r$ by a power $p^k$ of a small prime just large enough to clear $a_{i-1}$ yields $x<a_i$ once $q$ exceeds the small-prime product — this is exactly the `aimo-0030` "comparison of products of distinct prime factors" move. Rigorize the inequality.
     e. **Admissibility transfer (LOAD-BEARING SUB-LEMMA):** $x$ is admissible against $\mathcal F_{i-1}$. For each $a_j$ ($j<i$), $a_i$ shares some prime $r_j$ with $a_j$ (pairwise intersection). If $r_j\le M_1$, then $r_j\mid A\mid x$, so $x$ hits $a_j$. **The obstruction:** some $a_j$ might share ONLY $q$ with $a_i$ (so $x$ misses $a_j$). RULE THIS OUT: $T\setminus\{q\}$ is a transversal of $\mathcal F_{i-1}$ (since $q$ is private to $a_i$), so every $a_j$ is hit by a $T\setminus\{q\}$-prime — but those primes might not lie in $S(a_i)$. USE the greedy coupling ($d_n\le M_1$, every term lands in $\mathcal B_\infty$, cyclic-successor structure from Lemma 4) to force the shared primes into the small regime. This is the one real obstacle; without it the strip is just the `aimo-0030` skeleton.
     f. **Contradiction.** $x$ admissible, $a_{i-1}<x\le a_i$, and $x\ne a_i$ ( $x$ has only primes $\le M_1$, $a_i$ has $q>M_1$). Greedy minimality gives $a_i\le x$, contradicting $x<a_i$. Hence no governing $q>M_1$.
  4. **Fallback sub-mechanism A (opening 2, least-multiplier minimality):** if the admissibility-transfer step stumbles, attack the multiplier $m=a_i/p$ ($p\in P_1\cap S(a_i)$) directly. $m$ is the LEAST integer reaching the threshold $a_{i-1}$ with the required prime signature; minimality of $m$ (not just of $a_i$) may pin $q\mid m$ to $q\le M_1$ without needing the full strip.
  5. **Fallback sub-mechanism B (opening 4, witness recurrence + gap bound):** a governing $q$ must be re-witnessed infinitely often (else transient). Consecutive re-witnesses $a_{i_k},a_{i_{k+1}}$ are distinct multiples of $q$, so $a_{i_{k+1}}-a_{i_k}\ge q>M_1\ge$ (per-step gap); the witness density $\le M_1/q<1$ per step. Pair with a covering-capacity bound (see `witness-density-recurrence` for the standalone version) to derive $q\le M_1\cdot C$. Use as a last resort if the one-shot strip fails.
  6. **Endgame (certified).** With all governing primes $\le M_1$: $G\subseteq\{p\le M_1\}$ finite. Distinct supports (subsets of primes $\le M_1$ appearing in some $a_i$) form an increasing bounded family $\Rightarrow$ stabilize. $\operatorname{MT}$ depends only on the set-system, so $\operatorname{MT}(\mathcal F_n)$ stabilizes $\Rightarrow$ $\mathcal B_\infty$ is $L$-periodic. By Lemmas 4–6, $a_{n+T}=a_n+L$ for all $n\ge1$.
  7. LOCK sub-case: Lemma 9 (certified).
Key lemmas (claim + one-line mechanism):
  - Strip size bound $x=p^k A\le a_i=qA'$ — because $q>M_1\ge$ (small-prime product of $a_i$), and the `aimo-0030` product comparison (replace the large factor $q$ by the least power of a small prime clearing $a_{i-1}$) gives $x<a_i$.
  - Admissibility transfer — for each $j<i$, $a_i$ shares a SMALL prime $\le M_1$ with $a_j$, because $T\setminus\{q\}$ transverses $\mathcal F_{i-1}$ AND the greedy coupling ($d_n\le M_1$, terms in $\mathcal B_\infty$, cyclic-successor structure) forces shared primes into the small regime. **LOAD-BEARING.**
Open gaps: Step 3d (rigorous size inequality), Step 3e (admissibility transfer — the single load-bearing sub-lemma).
Cases to cover: LOCK sub-case (certified, Lemma 9); NO-LOCK general regime (the strip).
Watch out for: transient-vs-governing distinction (transient primes can be huge — do NOT bound primes in $\operatorname{MT}(\mathcal F_n)$ at finite $n$); the star counterexample $\{\{1,j\}:j\ge2\}$ shows the abstract pairwise-intersecting structure admits unbounded transversal primes — the greedy coupling is ESSENTIAL, not optional.

## prime-power-dichotomy: advance
Target: the whole theorem, via the LOCK/NO-LOCK dichotomy.
Technique: dichotomy (LOCK certified via `lock-lemma`); NO-LOCK redirects to the MT-prime bound $q\le M_1$ using the $|S(a_i)|\ge2$ structure for a sharpened strip. Distinct-supports-stabilize replaces the flawed MT-monotonicity.
Skeleton:
  1. Import certified foundation: linchpin + gap bound, pairwise-intersecting.
  2. **LOCK branch (certified).** Import `lock-lemma`: if some $a_i=p^k$, then $a_{n+1}=a_n+p$ for all $n\ge1$, so $T=1,L=p$. Verified for $a_1\in\{6,21,33,50\}$.
  3. **NO-LOCK branch setup.** Assume no $a_i$ is a prime power $\Rightarrow$ every $|S(a_i)|\ge2$ (in particular $|P_1|\ge2$). Set up the transversal framework $\mathcal B_n=\bigcup_{T\in\operatorname{MT}(\mathcal F_n)}\{\operatorname{rad}(T)\mid m\}$ as in C.1 (already written).
  4. **Fix C.3 (the flawed MT-monotonicity claim).** DELETE " $\operatorname{MT}(\mathcal F_n)$ is a non-increasing antichain; adding a set can only remove elements." This is FALSE (counterexample $F=\{\{1,2\}\}$, add $\{2,3\}$, gains $\{1,3\}$; the round-1 per-role rule records this). REPLACE with the **distinct-supports-stabilize** argument: once Gap C holds (finitely many MT-primes, all $\le$ some bound $B$), the distinct supports $\{S(a_i):i\ge1\}$ are subsets of primes $\le B$ — an increasing bounded family ($\le2^{\pi(B)}$ subsets) $\Rightarrow$ stabilize at some $N_0$. $\operatorname{MT}$ depends only on the set-system (a standard fact: minimal transversals are determined by the distinct member-sets, not multiplicities or order), so $\operatorname{MT}(\mathcal F_n)=\operatorname{MT}(\mathcal F_\infty)$ for $n\ge N_0$.
  5. **Import `greedy-equals-cyclic-successor` (certified lemma).** This closes Gap B (pure-from-start) UNCONDITIONALLY: $a_{n+1}=\min(\mathcal B_\infty\cap(a_n,\infty))$ for all $n\ge1$. Delete the open Gap B marker in C.4 — it is resolved by the certified lemma.
  6. **Redirect NO-LOCK to the MT-prime bound $q\le M_1$ (Gap C).** In the NO-LOCK regime, $|S(a_i)|\ge2$ for every $i$. Run the minimal-criminal strip (same `aimo-0030` mechanism as `transversal-saturation` Step 3), sharpened by the size-$\ge2$ structure: the witness $a_i$ (with $S(a_i)\cap T=\{q\}$, $q>M_1$) has $|S(a_i)|\ge2$, so besides $q$ it carries at least one OTHER prime, and by the linchpin at least one $p\in P_1\cap S(a_i)$ with $p\ne q$. The strip, size bound, and contradiction go through as in `transversal-saturation`. Mechanism: `aimo-0030` product comparison + the $|S|\ge2$ anchor.
  7. **Do NOT lean on "every $P_1$-prime recurs" for $|P_1|\ge3$.** Explorer-3 confirmed: the lemma holds empirically (zero counterexamples in 694 no-lock $|P_1|\ge3$ cases) but has NO proof mechanism for $|P_1|\ge3$ (a $P_1$-prime can go silent for hundreds of terms without forcing a lock — $a_1=2085$: prime 139 silent for ~180 terms, no lock through 6000 terms). So the NO-LOCK $|P_1|\ge3$ regime relies on the MT-prime bound strip (Step 6), NOT on the recurrence lemma.
  8. **Endgame (certified).** Gap C closed ($q\le M_1$) $\Rightarrow$ distinct supports stabilize (Step 4) $\Rightarrow$ $\operatorname{MT}$ stabilizes $\Rightarrow$ $\mathcal B_\infty$ $L$-periodic $\Rightarrow$ import `cyclic-successor-bijection` (certified) $\Rightarrow$ $a_{n+T}=a_n+L$ for all $n\ge1$.
Key lemmas (claim + one-line mechanism):

  - MT-prime bound $q\le M_1$ in NO-LOCK — the `aimo-0030` strip with the $|S(a_i)|\ge2$ anchor strengthening admissibility transfer (the second small prime gives more shared-prime options).
Open gaps: Step 6 (the strip's admissibility transfer — same load-bearing step as `transversal-saturation`, sharpened but not eliminated by $|S|\ge2$).
Cases to cover: LOCK (certified); NO-LOCK $|P_1|=2$ (covered by LOCK via dropout$\Rightarrow$lock); NO-LOCK $|P_1|\ge3$ (the strip).
Watch out for: do NOT invoke MT-monotonicity (the $\{\{1,2\}\}\to\{2,3\}$ counterexample); do NOT invoke "every $P_1$-prime recurs" for $|P_1|\ge3$ (no mechanism); the "dropout$\Rightarrow$lock" lemma is valid only for $|P_1|=2$ and is subsumed by LOCK.

## growing-modulus-descent: revise

Technique: `aimo-0678` "min-of-a-set monovariant + finite-state-lift" (crux: `invariants-and-monovariants` / `size-bounding-and-descent` / `modular-arithmetic-and-CRT`).   2. **KILL the typed-rank framing (Gap D as typed descent).** Explorer-3 confirmed: the witness type $\tau(q)=(A(q),B(q))\in2^{P_1}\times2^{P_1}$ is intrinsically too coarse — at $a_1=385$, primes $2,3,13$ are pulled in SIMULTANEOUSLY at $n=2$ with IDENTICAL type $(\{5\},\{5,7,11\})$; the finest $P_1$-interaction type also fails to distinguish them. RETIRE Gap D as typed descent.
  3. **NEW Gap D: the `aimo-0678` min-of-a-set monovariant.** Two-phase structure mirroring `aimo-0678`:
     a. **Phase 1 — freeze the $P_1$-structure (the `aimo-0678` "freeze $s_n$ during the divisibility phase" analogue).** The $P_1$-support family $\Sigma_n=\{S(a_i)\cap P_1:i\le n\}\subseteq2^{P_1}$ is increasing and bounded ($\le2^{|P_1|}$ subsets), so it stabilizes UNCONDITIONALLY at some $N_0$: $\Sigma_\infty=\Sigma_{N_0}$. After $N_0$, every new term's $P_1$-support is already in the fixed finite family $\Sigma_\infty$. (This is the free F3 observation — trivial as a standalone, but it is the frozen invariant the monovariant needs.)
     b. **Phase 2 — the monovariant on the large-prime frontier (the `aimo-0678` "$w_n=\min\{m\ge a_n:m\nmid s_n\}$" analogue).** After $N_0$, define a CONCRETE integer monovariant:
        $$w_n:=\min\bigl\{\,\text{primes }q>M_1:\ q\in\textstyle\bigcup_{T\in\operatorname{MT}(\mathcal F_n)}T\,\bigr\},\quad\text{with }w_n:=+\infty\text{ if the set is empty}.$$
        I.e. $w_n$ is the smallest MT-prime exceeding $M_1$ at step $n$ (the "frontier" — the smallest large prime the configuration currently REQUIRES). This is a min-of-a-set, exactly in the `aimo-0678` style.
     c. **MONOTONICITY (the load-bearing sub-lemma, the `aimo-0678` step to mirror).** $w_n$ is NON-INCREASING for $n\ge N_0$. Mechanism (to mirror `aimo-0678`'s "$w_{n+1}\le w_n$ because the lcm term is divisible by $a_n$ but the gcd term is not"): once the family $\Sigma_\infty$ is COVERED by primes $\le q$ (i.e., every $P_1$-support in $\Sigma_\infty$ is hit by some prime $\le q$ that lies in the current MT-prime set), adding more terms (whose $P_1$-supports are already in $\Sigma_\infty$, hence already covered) CANNOT create a new requirement for a prime $>q$ — the existing covering primes $\le q$ still hit every new support. So the smallest required large prime can only DROP (when a smaller large prime gets pulled in) or STAY (when the current frontier suffices); it can never INCREASE, because an increase would mean a previously-covered support is now uncovered, which the frozen $\Sigma_\infty$ forbids. [The builder must make this "covered stays covered" argument rigorous — it is the analogue of `aimo-0678`'s monotonicity proof and the one real difficulty.]
     d. **BOUND and TERMINATION.** $w_n\ge\min\{q\text{ prime}:q>M_1\}$ (or $w_n=+\infty$). A non-increasing integer sequence bounded below stabilizes: $w_n\to w_\infty$ at some finite step $N_1$. After $N_1$, the frontier is constant.
     e. **RESIDUAL CASE $w_\infty<+\infty$.** If $w_\infty=+\infty$, no large prime is ever required $\Rightarrow$ all MT-primes $\le M_1$ $\Rightarrow$ Gap A closed. If $w_\infty=q^*<+\infty$ (some prime $q^*>M_1$ is permanently required), apply the `aimo-0030` strip (from `transversal-saturation` Step 3) to rule out $q^*$ — the strip is a one-shot contradiction that handles any individual offending prime. [FLAG: this residual case leans on the strip as a secondary mechanism; the monovariant alone may not eliminate $q^*$.]
  4. **Endgame (certified).** Gap D closed $\Rightarrow$ $G\subseteq\{p\le M_1\}$ finite $\Rightarrow$ distinct supports stabilize $\Rightarrow$ $\operatorname{MT}$ stabilizes $\Rightarrow$ $\mathcal B_\infty$ $L$-periodic $\Rightarrow$ import `cyclic-successor-bijection` $\Rightarrow$ $a_{n+T}=a_n+L$ for all $n\ge1$.
  5. LOCK sub-case: import `lock-lemma` (certified).
Key lemmas (claim + one-line mechanism):
  - $P_1$-support stabilization (Phase 1) — $\Sigma_n$ stabilizes at $N_0$, because it is an increasing sequence in the finite lattice $2^{P_1}$. [Unconditional, free.]
  - Monovariant monotonicity (Phase 2, LOAD-BEARING) — $w_n$ non-increasing for $n\ge N_0$, because once $\Sigma_\infty$ is covered by primes $\le q$, adding terms with $P_1$-supports in $\Sigma_\infty$ cannot create a requirement for a prime $>q$ (the covering persists — the `aimo-0678` "covered stays covered" mechanism). Open gaps: Step 3c (monotonicity proof — the load-bearing step); Step 3e (the residual $w_\infty<+\infty$ case — needs the strip as a secondary mechanism).

## witness-density-recurrence: new
Target: the whole theorem, via a witness-recurrence + spacing/density argument on the infinite re-witnessing structure of governing primes (a COUNTING argument, NOT a one-shot minimal-criminal strip and NOT an algebraic monovariant — the field's third distinct mechanism for Gap A).
Technique: counting / density (`pigeonhole / extremal principle`, KB line 108/188) + `Vieta jumping & infinite descent` (KB line 83/184, the "no minimal counterexample" framing) on the witness-density of a governing prime.
Skeleton:
  1. Import certified foundation: linchpin + gap bound, pairwise-intersecting, every-term-in-$\mathcal B_\infty$, greedy-equals-cyclic-successor, cyclic-successor-bijection, lock-lemma.
  2. **Define GOVERNING vs TRANSIENT (without assuming Gap A — no circularity).** A prime $q$ is GOVERNING iff $q\in G_n:=\bigcup_{T\in\operatorname{MT}(\mathcal F_n)}T$ for infinitely many $n$ (appears in MT at arbitrarily large steps). $q$ is TRANSIENT iff $q\in G_n$ for some but only finitely many $n$ (drops out eventually). Gap A $=$ "only finitely many primes are governing."
  3. **Re-witnessing structure.** If $q$ is governing, there are infinitely many "witness steps" $n_k$ (with $q\in G_{n_k}$) and corresponding witness terms $a_{i_k}$ ($i_k\le n_k$) for which $q$ is PRIVATE: $S(a_{i_k})\cap T_k=\{q\}$ for some $T_k\in\operatorname{MT}(\mathcal F_{n_k})\ni q$ (standard characterization of minimal transversals: every element is private to some member). Each witness satisfies $q\mid a_{i_k}$.
  4. **Spacing constraint (gap bound + divisibility).** Consecutive witnesses $a_{i_k}<a_{i_{k+1}}$ are distinct multiples of $q$, so $a_{i_{k+1}}-a_{i_k}\ge q$. By the gap bound, $a_{i_{k+1}}-a_{i_k}\le(i_{k+1}-i_k)\cdot M_1$. Hence the witness INDEX gap $i_{k+1}-i_k\ge q/M_1$. If $q>M_1$ then $i_{k+1}-i_k\ge2$ (witnesses are non-adjacent), and the witness DENSITY is $\le M_1/q<1$ per step.
  5. **Density-incompatibility (LOAD-BEARING SUB-LEMMA).** A prime $q$ can remain governing only if its witness density is $\ge$ some threshold $\delta(q)>M_1/q$ (when $q>M_1$). I.e., the governance requirement forces frequent re-witnessing, incompatible with the $\le M_1/q$ spacing bound. Mechanism to develop: between consecutive witnesses $a_{i_k},a_{i_{k+1}}$, the terms $a_{i_k+1},\dots,a_{i_{k+1}-1}$ are added to $\mathcal F_n$; each new term's support must be hit by some MT containing $q$ (else $q$ leaves $\operatorname{MT}$ and becomes transient). The number of new supports that can be "absorbed" without re-witnessing $q$ is bounded by the COVERING CAPACITY of $T\setminus\{q\}$ — the maximum number of distinct supports $T\setminus\{q\}$ can hit without $q$. If the covering capacity is $C$ (a function of $|P_1|$ and the MT structure), then $i_{k+1}-i_k\le C$, giving density $\ge1/C$. Combined with density $\le M_1/q$, this yields $q\le M_1\cdot C$. [The covering-capacity bound is the crux — the builder must show $C$ is finite and bounded by a function of $|P_1|$ alone.]
  6. **Finiteness of governing primes.** $q\le M_1\cdot C$ for every governing $q$ (with $C$ a $|P_1|$-dependent constant). The governing set $G\subseteq\{p\le M_1\cdot C\}$ (finite). [If $C$ can be pinned to $1$, this recovers the sharp $q\le M_1$.]
  7. **Endgame (certified).** $G$ finite $\Rightarrow$ distinct supports stabilize $\Rightarrow$ $\operatorname{MT}$ stabilizes $\Rightarrow$ $\mathcal B_\infty$ $L$-periodic $\Rightarrow$ import `cyclic-successor-bijection` $\Rightarrow$ $a_{n+T}=a_n+L$ for all $n\ge1$.
  8. LOCK sub-case: import `lock-lemma` (certified).
Key lemmas (claim + one-line mechanism):
  - Re-witnessing — governing $\Rightarrow$ infinitely many witness terms with $q$ private, because $q\in G_n$ for infinitely many $n$ and at each such $n$, $q$ is private for some term (minimality of $\operatorname{MT}$). [Standard.]
  - Spacing — consecutive witnesses are multiples of $q$ with difference $\ge q$; index gap $\ge q/M_1$ by the gap bound. [Divisibility + $d_n\le M_1$.]
  - Density-incompatibility (LOAD-BEARING) — governance requires witness density $\ge1/C$ (covering capacity of $T\setminus\{q\}$); combined with density $\le M_1/q$, gives $q\le M_1\cdot C$. [The covering-capacity argument is the crux; $C$ must be finite and $|P_1|$-bounded.]
Open gaps: Step 5 (density-incompatibility — the covering capacity $C$ of $T\setminus\{q\}$ must be bounded by a function of $|P_1|$; this is the single load-bearing step).
Cases to cover: LOCK sub-case (certified); general case via the density argument.
Watch out for: the transient-vs-governing distinction (transient primes don't need re-witnessing — don't count them); the covering capacity $C$ must be FINITE and bounded by $|P_1|$ (else no finite bound on $q$); the star counterexample $\{\{1,j\}:j\ge2\}$ shows the abstract pairwise-intersecting family has UNBOUNDED covering capacity (each new $\{1,j\}$ needs a fresh prime) — the greedy coupling ($d_n\le M_1$, terms in $\mathcal B_\infty$) is ESSENTIAL to bound $C$.
