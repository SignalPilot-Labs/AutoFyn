NEVER: accept a "covering capacity C of T\{q} is bounded by |P_1|" argument without checking circularity — the star {{1,j}:j>=2} and projective planes show C is unbounded for abstract pairwise-intersecting families, and bounding the distinct supports between witnesses IS Gap A itself (witness-density-recurrence round 2's crux was circular).
ALWAYS: when all live approaches share one wall, name the SINGLE shared sub-claim explicitly in the report (here: "the greedy coupling tames the abstract unboundedness of pairwise-intersecting transversal primes" — the strip's admissibility-transfer, the monovariant's covered-stays-covered, and the density's covering-capacity are all costumes of this one claim) so the orchestrator can force a genuinely-different framing next round if they stall (round 2).
ALWAYS: distinguish the TWO notions of "transient" when a minimal-criminal/Schur approach relies on "transient primes die in cofactors" — MT-transient (drops out of MT(F_inf), what minimal-criminal on smallest governing q gives) is NOT the same as cofactor-transient (stops dividing k_i = a_n/q, what Schur needs); a prime can divide infinitely many a_n (hence infinitely many k_i at q-multiple indices) while not governing. Verify the bridge between the two notions is a real lemma, not an unstated assumption (because imo-2026-06 minimal-criminal-schur-contradiction round 4's transient-death crux hangs on exactly this gap; my computation showed 136/242 q-multiples for a1=15,q=3 carry a cofactor-prime > M_1=15, so the Schur premise fails as literally stated, round 4).
NEVER: accept a primal-vs-dual framing as a "bypass" of Gap A when the outliner itself admits equivalence — the admissibility check "does S(m)={q}∪primefactors(k) hit every current minimal" unpacks to bounding primefactors(k), which IS the cofactor bound certified dead. Require the builder to exhibit a window-local ingredient (e.g. "at most ONE q-multiple in the window of size M_1 < q" + greedy-minimality asking whether that unique q-multiple is the SMALLEST) that goes beyond "is the q-multiple admissible," or treat as Gap-A-restated (imo-2026-06 primal-minimal-support-stabilization round 4).
ALWAYS: in a deep stall (3+ rounds, 10+ dead mechanisms), accept a genuinely-new approach with an explicit negative-lemma fallback even if its crux is open/possibly-equivalent-to-Gap-A — the negative lemma (e.g. "primal ≡ Gap A," "no Schur-style contradiction ports") fences off future retries and IS the round's contribution. Do NOT cut such an approach as Gap-A-restated if its contradiction SHAPE is genuinely new (not in the dead list); register it CHANGES REQUESTED with the fallback mandated (imo-2026-06 round 4, both new approaches).

ALWAYS: cite the new T-unbounded-in-M_1 impossibility (rad-77 witness: a_1=77→T=18 vs a_1=847→T=1744 at the same M_1=77) as the general fence against ANY f(M_1)-bounded forward-deterministic statistic σ with σ→d_{n+1} single-valued — it has ≥T realized values, T unbounded in M_1. This subsumes and sharpens the round-3 modular-residue fence and the round-4 ergodic-window/increment-automaton fences into one general fence against the entire finite-pigeonhole-state class (round 5, minimal-counterexample explorer).

NEVER: approve a "two-coincidence ⇒ periodicity" framing without resolving the mechanism confusion — for a forward-single-valued deterministic abstraction α, ONE self-coincidence already gives α-periodicity by induction (the "second coincidence" is redundant for α); the second coincidence only earns its keep if α does NOT determine d_{n+1}, in which case the lift (α-periodicity → d_n-periodicity) is the load-bearing unproved mechanism, NOT "trivial for finite-state / hard for infinite-state." The obstacle is non-determinism, not infiniteness (round 5, two-coincidence-periodicity review).

ALWAYS: check whether a "no finiteness assumption" claim in an outline is actually true — pigeonhole-on-finite-alphabet IS a finiteness assumption (the alphabet size is f(M_1)), and the T-unbounded-in-M_1 impossibility kills it if the statistic is forward-deterministic-determining. An approach that claims to "use no finiteness" but invokes pigeonhole on |P_1| is making an f(M_1)-bounded finiteness assumption (round 5, two-coincidence-periodicity review).

NEVER: build an approach whose central lemma is "exhibit a finite X from a NON-f(M_1) source" without the source being identified — that is a restatement of Gap A in different language, not a route past it (round 5, augmented-bijective-state RETHINK).

NEVER: build an approach whose finish step is the reduce-mod-lcm modular-residue statistic (FENCED: minimal functional modulus = L) without a non-fenced alternative finish identified (round 5, frozen-radical-monovariant RETHINK).

ALWAYS: when an approach mounts on certified prior setup (e.g. minimal-criminal Steps 1–6) with a NEW Step-7 mechanism, verify the new mechanism is genuinely different from the fenced Step-7 (Schur, cofactor) — the mount being certified does NOT make the new mechanism sound (round 5, deviation-index-descent review).

ALWAYS: re-run any computational witness cited by an explorer before APPROVING a lemma for certification — the realized-state count may match while a derivative count (conflicts, conflicts-with-repeats) does not (round 6, no-fixed-modulus-forward-determinism: explorer reported 3498 conflicts, my independent MT-greedy run gave 4611 at N=60000 / 4447 at N=50000 with realized=9625 matching exactly; the qualitative claim held but the exact number was not reproducible — flag as a CHANGES note to the builder, not a RETHINK).
NEVER: cite an a1²-modulus "forward-deterministic=True" row for a1=847 (state space 717409 ≫ N) as evidence — it is a coverage artifact (realized=N-1, no state has repeated yet, trivially single-successor), not forward-determinism; confirm forward-determinism needs N > a1² (round 6, mt_greedy.py verification).

ALWAYS: when probing a "window-statistic determines next increment" claim, check that the probe targets d_{n+1} (the NEXT increment), NOT d_n = min D_n (which is tautologically determined by D_n by the greedy's definition). I caught my own probe checking the wrong target in round 7 — the tautology gave 0 conflicts and a false-positive forward-determinism result; switching to d_{n+1} still gave 0 conflicts for most cases but surfaced the real a_1=175 exception (round 7).

ALWAYS: when a forward-determinism probe shows "1 conflict at every k from 1..K", trace the conflict pair before declaring k_* unbounded — it is often a SINGLE forced step (|D_n|=1, where the singleton doesn't encode a_n's value) colliding with periodic forced steps; the backward-D-match depth is finite (the transient separates from the periodic at the preperiod boundary), so k_* = max-depth+1 is finite. a_1=175 showed this: 1 conflict at every k=1..15, but it separated at k=16 (round 7).

ALWAYS: the rad-77 impossibility fence ("T not a function of M_1") bites ONLY when the realized forward-deterministic state count is ~T (comparable to the period). A statistic with state space 2^{k·M_1} (astronomically larger than T) clears the fence even at k=1, because pigeonhole gives period ≤ 2^{k·M_1} ≫ T with no contradiction. The fence is about MINIMAL state size ~T, not about arbitrary f(M_1)-bounded bounds. So a finite-k non-residue window statistic with realized states ≪ T genuinely clears rad-77 (round 7, f-of-a1 probe: a_1=847 realized=192 ≪ T=1744, 0 conflicts at k=1).

ALWAYS: the D_n-window (set-valued admissible-increment tuple) is STRICTLY richer than the fenced single-value d_n-window — empirically confirmed (round 7): d_n-window has conflicts at every k≤5 for the hard cases (reproducing the round-4 fence), while D_n-window is forward-deterministic at k=1 for {15,35,77,91,847,375} and at k=16 for a_1=175. The distinction the outline draws (D_n retains the |D_n|≥2 slack that d_n=min D_n projects away) is REAL, not verbal. The one caveat: forward-determinism can fail at FORCED steps (|D_n|=1, where the slack is absent), requiring a longer finite window.
