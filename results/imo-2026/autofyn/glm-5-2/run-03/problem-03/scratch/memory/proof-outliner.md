# proof-outliner role rules

ALWAYS: when the lower bound's key lemma (e.g. splits-inequality / alternating-sum invariant) is shared across multiple approaches, flag it as the highest-leverage gap to certify first as a shared lemma in `results/<id>/lemmas/` — once certified it benefits the whole field. (Round 1, imo-2026-03: G1 splits-inequality lemma shared by all 4 approaches.)

ALWAYS: check the surrogate-adversary DIRECTION carefully. For an UPPER bound (cap the maximizer), the surrogate must be a WEAKER/RESTRICTED minimizer whose win transfers UP to the real minimizer — the opposite of the lower-bound surrogate direction (aimo-0560's gardener/lumberjack is a LOWER-bound engine: stronger minimizer, win transfers down). Getting this backwards kills the argument. (Round 1, imo-2026-03.)

NEVER: assume the naive dyadic telescope `Σ 1/2^k = 1 − 1/2^n` directly gives the target when the target is `1/D_n = 1/(2^{n+1}−1) ≈ 1/2^{n+1}` — there is a factor-of-2 gap between `1/2^n` and `1/2^{n+1}` that must be explicitly resolved (either via a tighter initial bound `D_0 < 1` or scaled decrements). Papering over this gap is a silent bug. (Round 1, imo-2026-03 alternating-potential.)

ALWAYS: read every explorer report before outlining — each explorer scouts a different framing and their openings are raw material for distinct rival approaches. The consensus answer + recursion is strong but the BOTTLENECK is usually one half (here the upper bound); diversify the field on the bottleneck half, not the easy half. (Round 1, imo-2026-03.)

ALWAYS: when an approach has a CONFIRMED dead-end crux (reviewer-verified factor-of-2 obstacle), do NOT re-outline the same crux with a patch — pivot the approach to a genuinely different framing or recommend retirement/merge. Patches to a dead framing share the same wall and die together. (Round 2, imo-2026-03: alternating-potential's direct-D-cap pivoted to amortized-potential on parity-XOR; surrogate-adversary re-planned to minimax-strategy-family.)

NEVER: use the naive "a split of p into α+(p−α) only ADDS to j(t)" reading of the parity-integral — a split ADDS +1 to j on [0,α] AND SUBTRACTS −1 on [p−α,p]. Missing the subtract gives wrong j-profiles and wrong D-values; the subtract is load-bearing. (Round 2, imo-2026-03, g1-lower explorer.)

ALWAYS: prove convexity-of-order-statistics from scratch — the k-th largest element = max of linear forms over k-subsets, hence convex; S_odd = sum of odd-order statistics is convex. This technique is NOT in knowledge_base.md; the builder must invoke it explicitly. (Round 2, imo-2026-03, g1-lower explorer Route A.)

NEVER: let two upper-bound approaches share the SAME strengthened-hypothesis gap (here "the derived-rest hypothesis: the rest after a peeling split is not arbitrary, its largest piece is p_1−p_j inheriting Liu's structure") — if that hypothesis fails, both approaches die together (the single-gap trap). Pairing-charging's direct partition and alternating-potential's amortized potential both lean on it; flag the dependency so the outliner can break the tie if one fails. (Round 2, imo-2026-03.)

ALWAYS: when a shared open gap (e.g. the G1 overlap bound `2C ≥ D_{R_0}+D_F+1−M`) has been re-derived by an explorer as an equivalent restatement (union-measure form, band-parity form, LP dual form), do NOT frame the re-derivation as a NEW rival approach — it is the same wall in different words. The win, if any, comes only from the re-derivation making a sub-case's bookkeeping cleaner; route the re-lens to the approach owning that sub-case, and keep the field's genuinely-orthogonal openings (e.g. minimax-on-Liu's-side collapse theorem) as the actual new slugs. (Round 4, imo-2026-03: measure-extremizer Opening A and telescoping-potential Opening C are re-lenses of G1; only Opening B "worst Liu = dyadic" is a genuinely new G2 attack.)

ALWAYS: when a builder's casework has ballooned ("12 expressions × 3 sort-regimes"), look for the **piecewise-linear / polyhedral-vertex reduction** (KB: *Piecewise-concavity smoothing*) BEFORE accepting the casework as the wall — a min of PWL functions attains its max at an arrangement vertex (a crossing of two forms or a form-meets-facet), and a finite vertex table + a 2–3-case contradiction is the clean replacement. This is the standard structural kill for "too many cases" and generalizes the n=2 §6.3 contradiction to finer arrangements. (Round 5, imo-2026-03: pairing-charging Case-C 12-expression casework collapses to a finite vertex check, unique maximizer at the dyadic fixed point.)

NEVER: over-generalize an "LP-dual is dead" verdict from the STACKELBERG-BLIND LP (Liu best-responds to a fixed mixed strategy; has an integrality gap = value of Xiang's information) to the PER-REGION LP (Liu fixed first; cut-space partitioned by sort-order; D linear per region; NO integrality gap). These are two different LPs; only the per-region one is relevant to a perfect-info upper bound. (Round 5, imo-2026-03: the round-4 "LP-dual dead" verdict was over-broad; the per-region LP-dual is a genuine fresh G2 framing.)

ALWAYS: when a finite strategy family fails by a small residual on a continuous problem (e.g. 0.68% short, worst config identified), check whether the binding continuous optimum sits at a DEGENERATE vertex the finite family structurally misses (here: cross-piece equal pairs — a fragment of `p_i` equaling `p_j`, realizable by an UNEQUAL split but not by any within-piece-only operation). The finite-family failure is often the signature that the right framing is continuous/LP-dual from the start, not a bigger finite family. (Round 5, imo-2026-03: minimax 14-member family fails worst config `(0.4545,0.2727,0.1818,0.0909)`; continuous opt `D=0` via two cross-piece equal pairs.)

ALWAYS: when a "genuinely-different framing" is proposed (e.g. F_2-reachability vs alternating-discrepancy, parity-coverage vs measure-inequality), flag the COLLAPSE-TO-RE-LENS check as a load-bearing verification the builder must do BEFORE building the full argument — two framings that ask different-sounding questions (reachability in F_2-toggle-algebra vs a union-measure inequality) may coincide at the bookkeeping level. If they coincide, the "new" framing is a re-lens (route to the owning approach, do not build as a rival slug); if not, it is a genuinely different attack. The explorer's caveat "it may still collapse" is the signal to flag this check. (Round 6, imo-2026-03: the parity-XOR-reachability G1 framing flagged with an explicit collapse-to-Lemma-7-union-bound check step.)

NEVER: over-generalize a "per-region LP is alive" verdict to "per-region LP-dual is a general-n bypass" — the per-region LP DUAL (the certificate) may collapse to the SAME finite-strategy family the direct construction already has (continuous optimum at EH+EH vertices = finite-strategy points). The per-region LP is sound SETUP (D linear per region, no integrality gap), but if its dual reduces to the finite pairwise-diff/peel/EH-n-largest family, it does NOT yield a new certificate for general-n. (Round 6, imo-2026-03: lp-dual-region's per-region LP dual confirmed to collapse to pairing-charging's finite family; lp-dual-region is a dead end for general-n bypass, kept only as a lemma donor.)
