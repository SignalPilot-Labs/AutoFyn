ALWAYS: for confluence/joinability approaches, run a quick python reduct-graph to check WCR is multi-step joinable (common reduct before terminal) vs only-at-terminal — distinguishes viable from circular (imo-2026-01, round 1).
NEVER: approve a 3-cell/local-confluence joinability lemma proved by "both reach a normal form and normal forms are unique" — that is circular; demand an explicit common reduct or a direct prime-by-prime invariant certificate (imo-2026-01, round 1).
