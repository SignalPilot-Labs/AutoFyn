# Lemma ρ (spiral similarity) — CERTIFIED (round 1, proof-reviewer)

**Statement.** The spiral similarity ρ centred at A with rotation angle ∠BAC (oriented
from ray AB to ray AC) and ratio AC/AB = b/c sends B ↦ C and M ↦ N (M,N midpoints of
AB,AC).

**Proof.** By definition ρ rotates ray AB onto ray AC and scales by AC/AB, so ρ(B) = C.
Since AM = c/2, AN = b/2, the ratio AN/AM = b/c equals ρ's ratio, and ray AM = ray AB is
carried by ρ onto ray AC = ray AN; hence ρ(M) = N. ∎

Elementary and correct. Note ρ(K) ≠ L in general.
