## Lemma: coordinate reduction OM=ON ⟺ O_x=p/2, and circumcenter formula

**Source approach:** coordinate-trig-bash (round 1). Certified by proof-reviewer, round 1.

### Setup
Place `B=(-1,0)`, `C=(1,0)`, `A=(p,q)` with `q>0` (WLOG for any triangle ABC,
by similarity: translate/rotate/scale/reflect so B,C land at (∓1,0) and A is
above line BC — OM, ON scale together, so OM=ON is scale/similarity-invariant).
Then `M=(A+B)/2 = ((p-1)/2, q/2)`, `N=(A+C)/2 = ((p+1)/2, q/2)`.

### Lemma 1 (reduction)
`OM = ON ⟺ O_x = p/2`.

*Proof.* `OM^2-ON^2 = (O-M)·(O-M) - (O-N)·(O-N) = 2O·(N-M) + |M|^2-|N|^2`.
Here `N-M=(1,0)` and `|M|^2-|N|^2 = [(p-1)^2-(p+1)^2]/4 = -p`. So
`OM^2-ON^2 = 2O_x - p`, which vanishes iff `O_x = p/2`. ∎

(Verified independently by the proof-reviewer via direct numeric substitution:
for `O_x=p/2`, `OM-ON=0` to machine precision for arbitrary `O_y`; for
`O_x≠p/2`, `OM≠ON`.)

### Lemma 2 (circumcenter formula)
For non-collinear `A=(ax,ay)`, `K=(kx,ky)`, `L=(lx,ly)`, the circumcenter
`O=(Ox,Oy)` of triangle AKL satisfies
$$O_x = \frac{|A|^2(k_y-l_y) + |K|^2(l_y-a_y) + |L|^2(a_y-k_y)}{D}, \quad D = 2[a_x(k_y-l_y)+k_x(l_y-a_y)+l_x(a_y-k_y)]$$
(D is twice the signed area of AKL, nonzero for a genuine triangle).

*Proof.* `|O-A|^2=|O-K|^2` and `|O-A|^2=|O-L|^2` expand to the linear system
`2O·(K-A) = |K|^2-|A|^2`, `2O·(L-A) = |L|^2-|A|^2` in unknowns `(Ox,Oy)`;
solve by Cramer's rule. ∎

### Status and scope
Both lemmas are elementary, fully rigorous, and reusable by any approach
working in this coordinate frame (e.g. complex-circle-power). They do NOT by
themselves solve the problem: the hard remaining step (deriving `O_x=p/2`,
equivalently Lemma 2's `Ox`, from the three angle hypotheses on K, L) is
still open — a concrete Gröbner-basis computation (independently reproduced
by the proof-reviewer with a from-scratch reconstruction of the angle
equalities, degree-2/3/3 polynomials in kx,ky,lx,ly, grevlex order) confirms
`O_x - p/2` is NOT in the ideal generated by the three raw angle-equality
polynomials alone — the target holds only on the branch selected by the
containment/orientation hypotheses, not on the full variety. This negative
finding is genuine and should not be re-attempted as a pure ideal-membership
computation without first isolating the correct real branch (e.g. via sign
conditions / semialgebraic methods, or by working with an explicit
parametrization all the way through).
