# Round 21 — 6th fresh-framing sweep on H1 (FAH / Symmetric FAH / Cofinite FAH / EEA)

**Verdict up front: still exhausted.** After checking every candidate the brief
suggested plus several more, and after locating one genuinely important *meta*-fact
in the certified lemma set that narrows what a "fresh framing" can even mean here, I
found no candidate with a real, non-circular foothold. This report is organized as:
(0) the meta-fact that should reshape how future rounds even look for a new framing,
(1) candidate-by-candidate (a)/(b)/(c) analysis, (2) one structural alternative
worth flagging even though it isn't a proof technique per se.

## 0. The load-bearing meta-fact: the Ambient-Statistic Obstruction is toolkit-independent

`lemmas/ambient-statistic-obstruction.md` (round 20, certified) proves something
stronger than its name suggests: **any** finite deductive argument whose premises
are all "ambient" — computed by a fixed formula in `X` and the rogue pair's fixed
finite data, without ever reading which integers are actually realized sequence
terms or their observed divisor-classes `g_n` — cannot settle a class-sensitive
conclusion (in particular cannot prove `E` finite). The proof is a one-line
determinism-of-deduction argument that never mentions a *specific* technique name.
That means it automatically kills not just the five named families in its scope
note (Mertens/sieve, escape-cost, density, and — as ambient variants — Borel–
Cantelli/2nd-moment/finite-Fourier/LP-relaxation) but **any future framing**,
however exotically named (coding theory, renewal theory, martingales, entropy
compression, Kolmogorov complexity, potential functions, …), *as long as it is built
from statistics that don't query the realized `g_n`/`ρ(n)` history*. This is why
essentially every "fresh vocabulary" sweep in rounds 13–20 collapsed to the same
wall: the wall isn't the vocabulary, it's ambient-ness, and the obstruction is
already a fully general filter for it.

Consequently, a genuinely new framing for H1 can only have a foothold if it is
**occupancy-referencing** (its premises actually read realized `a_n`/`ρ(n)`/`g_n`
data) — but the certified round-20 finding also flags, honestly, that the
occupancy-referencing versions of 2nd-moment/Borel–Cantelli/finite-Fourier/LP-
relaxation are *not yet formally ruled out*. I did not find a way to actually
execute any of them (see §1.2–1.4): once you demand the statistic reads realized
data, the argument becomes indistinguishable in substance from the gcd-pigeonhole
family already explored to exhaustion (Confined-GCD Lemma's `g_n ∈ Div(b)` alphabet,
Generalized Bounded Witness Lemma, etc.) — it just relabels the same finite-alphabet
walk with different terminology.

## 1. Candidates checked

### 1.1 Algorithmic information theory / Kolmogorov complexity
(a) Would say: if the gap/type sequence were not eventually periodic, some
description of it would need unbounded information beyond `a_1`, forcing a
contradiction with computability.
(b) Genuinely different vocabulary from anything on the dead list.
(c) **No foothold.** The sequence `(a_n)` is trivially computable from `a_1` alone
(the greedy rule is an explicit algorithm), so every prefix has Kolmogorov
complexity `O(log a_1) + O(log n)` *regardless* of whether it is eventually
periodic — computability alone never forces eventual periodicity (witness:
`⌊n√2⌋` is computable with the same complexity bound and is not eventually
periodic). To get traction you'd need to show the *specific* recursion here has an
extra finiteness property (e.g., that its state space collapses to a fixed finite
automaton) — but that is exactly EEA, already proved equivalent-difficulty, not a
bypass. Discarded: not a new route, it's a restatement of the automaton framing
with no new content.

### 1.2 Martingale / optional stopping on a different variable than Borel–Cantelli
(a) Would treat the indicator `1[ρ(n)=A', q*∤a_n]` (or the realized divisor-class
`g_n`) as if generated by a stochastic process and hope optional stopping forces
the "bad" event to have probability 0, hence (by some transfer) to occur finitely
often.
(b) Different named tool (stopping times vs. tail-sum Borel–Cantelli).
(c) **No real foothold, same wall as (0).** The sequence is fully deterministic;
there is no actual filtration/probability measure to attach a martingale to unless
one is invented (e.g. treating "which residue class gets hit next" as uniform over
some admissible set) — but the Selection-Rule Class-Blindness observation
(`sieve-density-exception-bound`, round 11, cited inside
`density-argument-vacuity-corollary.md`) already establishes that the actual
selection rule reads only `gcd(c,a_i)>1`, never which prime realizes it — so any
invented "random" model for which residue is hit is an ambient statistic by
construction (it can't be conditioned on the realized tail without begging the
question), and the Ambient-Statistic Obstruction applies verbatim. To make it
occupancy-referencing you'd have to define the "probability" using the actual
realized `g_n` sequence itself, at which point it stops being a martingale
argument and becomes exactly the (already exhaustively explored) gcd-pigeonhole
family tracking `g_n ∈ Div(b)` directly.

### 1.3 Renewal theory
(a) Would treat "recruitment events" (a new prime entering the core, or a
singleton-witness occurrence) as renewals and use elementary/Blackwell renewal
theorems to bound inter-renewal gaps, hence bound `N(S_k)` or produce a witness.
(b) Different name, and it targets termination/recurrence rather than a magnitude
squeeze.
(c) **Closest of the probabilistic candidates to being "new," but it targets H2
(absorption-chain termination), not H1 (FAH) as such** — and even for H2 it
reduces to the same non-constructive "two consistent finite-prefix extensions"
obstruction already proved in `core-growth-monotonicity` (round 16, Prop. 3):
renewal theorems need i.i.d.-ish or at least stochastically bounded inter-arrival
times, and nothing in the workspace establishes any distributional control on
"time to next recruitment" — that control is exactly the open content, not an
input renewal theory could supply for free. No foothold found for H1 specifically.

### 1.4 A genuinely different symbolic-dynamics encoding (not subword complexity)
Considered: Rauzy graphs / return words / bispecial factors (combinatorics on
words, used for *low-complexity* sequences like Sturmian words), and S-adic
representations.
(a) Would recast the gap sequence as a word over a finite alphabet and use
return-word structure to force eventual periodicity.
(b) Different specific machinery from the already-killed Morse–Hedlund/subword-
complexity attempt.
(c) **No foothold**: these techniques all *presuppose* the word already has
provably bounded factor complexity (that's their hypothesis, e.g. linear
complexity for Sturmian words) — establishing that bound here is exactly the
open content (equivalent to EEA per the certified round-12 finding), not a free
input. Same wall, different name; discarded per the brief's instruction not to
re-propose subword-complexity variants in any guise.

### 1.5 Coding theory / covering codes
(a) Would view `S₀ ∪ F''`-residue conditions as a covering code over `ℤ` and use
sphere-covering / Delsarte-type linear-programming bounds to force a
"dominant" prime.
(b) Different vocabulary from "CRT-glue/competitor-construction," but structurally
LP-duality relaxation is already on the dead list and covering-system-construction
(the actual covering-code-flavored approach already in the workspace) already
tried the CRT-glue mechanism to exhaustion (14th confirmed-dead mechanism, round
11, Minimal-Modulus Generalization). A code-theoretic reformulation of the same
covering-congruence data is not a new mechanism, just new nouns.
(c) No independent foothold beyond what's already dead.

### 1.6 Combinatorial game theory / strategy-stealing
(a) Would model the two competing persistent types `A'`, `B'` as "players"
racing to recruit a dominant prime, and use a strategy-stealing argument to show
one must eventually dominate.
(b) Genuinely unused vocabulary in this workspace.
(c) **No foothold — there is no game here.** The process is a single deterministic
sequence, not an alternating adversarial choice; there is no "move" to steal and
no symmetric strategy space to exploit. This looks attractive only because the
crux corpus's one `games-and-strategy` NT crux (`aimo-0779`, an actual two-player
game) has a superficially similar "make the same move as your opponent"
symmetrization flavor, but nothing in this problem has two agents. Checked the
crux corpus (`games-and-strategy` subtopic, NT and combinatorics) directly and
found no applicable pattern — these are all genuine games with alternating turns.

### 1.7 Occupancy-referencing 2nd-moment/finite-Fourier (the round-20-flagged residual)
Flagged in §0 as the one *formally* open corner. I attempted concretely to build
an occupancy-referencing 2nd-moment statistic: e.g. `V(N) := Var_{n≤N, ρ(n)=A'}
[1_{q*|a_n}]` computed from the *actual* realized sequence. The moment I write
this down honestly, computing `V(N)` requires knowing, for each realized
occurrence `n`, the value `g_n ∈ Div(b)` (Confined-GCD Lemma) — i.e. it's a
function of exactly the same finite-alphabet walk `(g_{n_1}, g_{n_2}, …)` that
`cofinite-window-capacity-bound` (round 9, stalled — "infinite pigeonhole yields
some infinite divisor-class but not exclusivity") already studies directly, with
no extra leverage gained from phrasing it as a "variance." Renaming the
finite-alphabet walk's statistics as a 2nd moment doesn't supply the missing
ingredient (a way to link `g_n` across different `n`, which `confined-gcd-lemma.md`
explicitly flags as absent from the certified toolkit). **Confirmed: not a new
route, it's the already-explored gcd-pigeonhole family wearing new terminology.**

## 2. One non-technique observation worth flagging to the outliner

Given that essentially every statistical/analytic/dynamical vocabulary transplant
collapses to the same two walls (ambient ⟹ killed by §0's obstruction;
occupancy-referencing ⟹ collapses into the already-exhausted gcd-pigeonhole
family), the productive place left for genuine diversity is probably NOT a new
top-level *technique* for proving FAH, but a serious, explicit attempt to
**construct a counterexample to FAH as currently formalized** (i.e., to show H1,
in its current "every two extended-persistent types eventually intersect on a
single confined prime" form, can genuinely fail for some `a_1`) — which would
force the run to route the whole IMO-P6 proof through a different top-level
architecture entirely (not core-absorption + FAH), rather than continuing to try
to prove a possibly-false reduction lemma. This is not a "framing" in the sense
the brief asked me to scout (it's adversarial to the current architecture, not a
new proof technique), so I am flagging it rather than developing it — the
outliner/outline-reviewer should decide whether a dedicated "refute H1 as stated"
approach is worth seating, since 15 rounds of failed proof attempts with zero
counterexamples found is itself ambiguous evidence (could mean "true but hard" or
"the formalization needs revising"), and no approach in `results/imo-2026-06/` is
currently dedicated to actively hunting a refutation on a systematically chosen
(not merely convenient) seed.

## Summary for the outliner

No new top-level framing with a concrete, non-circular foothold was found this
sweep. The single actionable finding is structural, not technical: the certified
Ambient-Statistic Obstruction (round 20) is toolkit-independent and pre-emptively
kills any future purely-ambient framing regardless of name, so future explorer
sweeps can skip re-checking "is this ambient?" case by case and instead ask
directly "does this framing's core step read the realized `g_n`/`ρ(n)` data?" — if
yes, it is (per this sweep) indistinguishable in substance from the exhausted
gcd-pigeonhole family; if no, it's killed outright. The one candidate that doesn't
fit this dichotomy is not a proof technique at all: a dedicated hunt for a genuine
FAH counterexample (§2), which would redirect effort away from the FAH-reduction
architecture altogether rather than trying to patch it a 21st time.
