ALWAYS: for two-stage adversarial "cut then claim" stick/interval problems, numerically stress-test any symmetric or repeated-value candidate construction against a "pairing exploit" — an adversary can often force an exact 50/50 split by bisecting just enough pieces to make the untouched leftover an even-count block of equal values; equal partitions and constructions with repeated piece sizes are almost always suboptimal for the first cutter (round 1, imo-2026-03).

ALWAYS: when a piece-claiming/item-alternation subgame appears (players alternately pick items to maximize own sum, no other constraints), verify computationally via bitmask game-tree DP that "always take the current largest" is optimal before assuming it — cheap to check (2000 random trials, <5s) and collapses a whole subgame to a closed form (round 1, imo-2026-03).
