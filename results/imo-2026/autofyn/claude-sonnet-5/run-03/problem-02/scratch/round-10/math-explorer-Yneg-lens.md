### 2. The EXACT precise remaining claim for (A) — corrected

Round 9's file states the target as simply "`2K-f(\beta_1)\ge0`" with no further hypothesis beyond `Y(\gamma)<0` and domain-nonemptiness `\beta_0<\gamma` (`\beta_0=(\pi-A)/3`). **I ran an independent, large-scale numeric check of exactly this literal claim and found it is FALSE as stated**: 35,519/80,555 violations in a 2,000,000-sample sweep (own script, `numpy`), with explicit witnesses, e.g. `A\approx0.2429,B\approx1.4159` gives `\beta_1\approx0.804`, `2K-f(\beta_1)\approx-0.10<0`.

**The resolution: a missing hypothesis was implicit but never stated.** In every single violation found, `\beta_0\ge\beta_1$ (i.e. the `(I)`-hypothesis window `\beta>\beta_0$ and the `(II)`-relevant window `\beta<\beta_1` from Case (b) are **disjoint**, so the sub-case is vacuous there — `(II)` doesn't need to hold at those triangles at all). Restricting to the genuinely non-vacuous regime `\beta_0<\beta_1$ (equivalently `\sin(A+3\beta_1)<0`, since `\sin(A+3\beta)$ is positive on `(0,\beta_0)$ and negative just past it), **the corrected claim holds with 0/29,548 violations** in an independent 2,000,000-sample sweep.

**Corrected precise statement (verified, not yet proved):**
$$\textbf{Claim (A′)}\quad 2\cos^2\beta_1\sin(A+B)=\sin B\cos A\ \wedge\ \sin(A+3\beta_1)<0\ \implies\ 2K-f(\beta_1)\ge0,$$
where `2K-f(\beta)=\sin B\sin(A+\beta)-2\sin(A+B)(\sin\beta-\sin A)$, `K=2\sin A\sin(A+B)`. This is a **sharper, corrected** version of the file's own Case (b) statement — the round-9 file's literal wording is an honest omission (it says "no further hypothesis needed (not even `\sin(A+3\beta)<0`)" for Case (a), and by parallel structure implicitly suggests the same is true for Case (b), which my numerics show is **not** the case). This correction should be surfaced to the outliner as load-bearing: **the extra hypothesis `\sin(A+3\beta_1)<0` (equivalently `\beta_1>\beta_0$) is not optional for Case (b).**

### 4. My own fresh algebraic experiments on Claim (A′)

**Setup.** Writing `x:=\cos\beta_1=\sqrt\lambda>0`, `y:=\sin\beta_1=\sqrt{1-\lambda}>0` (since `\beta_1\in(0,\pi/2)`, established because `\gamma=B<\pi/2` always under the standing WLOG), and using the already-certified `f=K+P\sin\beta+Q\cos\beta` decomposition (`P=\sin(A-B)/2+\tfrac32\sin(A+B)`, `Q=-\sin A\sin B`), I get
$$2K-f(\beta_1) = K+\sin A\sin B\,x - P\,y.$$
Since `x>0` and `K,\sin A\sin B>0`, the term `K+\sin A\sin B\,x =: \mathrm{expr}_1` is **always strictly positive**. So:
- **If `P\le0`:** target `\ge \mathrm{expr}_1>0` immediately — Claim (A′) holds trivially in this sub-case (verified: `-Py\ge0` when `P\le0`). *(New, cheap partial closure — worth stating as a lemma: `P\le0\implies` (A′) holds unconditionally, no need for the `\sin(A+3\beta_1)<0` hypothesis at all in this branch.)*
- **If `P>0`:** both `\mathrm{expr}_1>0` and `Py>0`; need `\mathrm{expr}_1\ge Py`. Squaring (valid since both sides nonnegative): need
$$D:=\mathrm{expr}_1^2-P^2y^2 = \mathrm{expr}_1^2-P^2(1-x^2)\ \ge\ 0.$$
`D` is a **quadratic polynomial in `x`** (not requiring `x=\sqrt\lambda$ eliminated): `D=A_{\mathrm{coef}}x^2+B_{\mathrm{coef}}x+C_{\mathrm{coef}}` with (own `sympy`, exact)
$$A_{\mathrm{coef}}=\sin^2A\sin^2B+P^2,\qquad B_{\mathrm{coef}}=4\sin^2A\sin B\sin(A+B),\qquad C_{\mathrm{coef}}=4\sin^2A\sin^2(A+B)-P^2.$$
**Key structural fact (verified, own derivation): `B_{\mathrm{coef}}>0` always** (product of manifestly positive triangle-trig quantities `\sin A,\sin B,\sin(A+B)`, all `>0` for genuine triangle angles). Since `x=\sqrt\lambda>0`, this means the "linear-in-`x`" part of `D` is **always a positive contribution**. Writing `E:=A_{\mathrm{coef}}\lambda+C_{\mathrm{coef}}` (the "even part", a genuine rational function of `A,B` alone, no `x`/`\sqrt{}$), `D = E + B_{\mathrm{coef}}\sqrt\lambda`.
- **If `E\ge0`: done, `D\ge0` trivially** (own numeric check: this covers roughly `27{,}074/29{,}654\approx91\%` of the corrected-domain sample space).
- **If `E<0` (the remaining `\approx9\%`, own numeric check: `2580/29654`): need `B_{\mathrm{coef}}^2\lambda\ge E^2`.** I computed this difference symbolically (own `sympy` session):
$$B_{\mathrm{coef}}^2\lambda - E^2 = \frac{8\sin^4A\sin^3B\sin^3(A+B)\cos A - \left[\text{(explicit degree-6-in-trig expression, displayed below)}\right]^2/16}{\sin^2(A+B)}$$
where the bracketed square-root argument is
$$-\bigl(\sin(A-B)+3\sin(A+B)\bigr)^2\sin(A+B) - \bigl(2\cos2A-\cos2B+2\cos(2A+2B)-3\bigr)\sin B\cos A + 16\sin^2A\sin^3(A+B).$$
This did **not** simplify further under `sympy.simplify`/`sp.factor` in the time available — it is a genuine, well-posed, purely trigonometric (no square roots) polynomial-in-`\sin/\cos` inequality in the two free variables `A,B`, confirmed numerically nonnegative in the `E<0` sub-regime (0 violations, consistent with the overall Claim (A′) numerics) but **not yet reduced to a clean closed form or proven**. This is the single most concrete open symbolic sub-target I can hand off.

**Net of this decomposition:** Claim (A′) is fully reduced (mechanically, exactly, via my own re-derivation) to: (i) the trivial case `P\le0` (closed); (ii) the `E\ge0` case (closed, ~91% of samples, purely by positivity of a rational trig expression — worth checking if `E\ge0` is itself provable symbolically as a clean sub-lemma, I did not attempt this); (iii) the residual `E<0` case, which needs the explicit degree-6 polynomial inequality above — a concrete Sturm/resultant/SOS target, not yet attempted symbolically.
