### New structural findings this round

**1. Rem is exactly σ-antisymmetric.** Applying the certified geometric symmetry
σ: B↔C, B̄↔C̄, h2↔h3 (h1 fixed) — the same σ used throughout the population
(`lemmas/sigma-symmetry.md`) — gives **Rem + σ(Rem) = 0 identically** (verified
symbolically, exact). This is a genuine consistency check: σ swaps K↔L, so
(A,K,L,Q)→(A,L,K,Q), a cross-ratio argument-swap that is known to preserve
"realness" but can flip the sign of the polynomial defining the real locus —
Rem's antisymmetry is exactly what's expected if the construction is correct,
and is a small new fact worth recording (not previously stated in the file)
but does not by itself close any gap.

**2. Clarified *why* Φ=0 carries only weak information.** I checked directly
(own `sympy`): Φ(h1,h2,h3,B,C) := D_pΔ − D_KD_L is **NOT** the zero polynomial
when h1,h2,h3,B,C are treated as fully free/independent symbols — it is a
genuine nontrivial algebraic identity that only holds when h1,h2,h3 actually
arise as H_i(K,L) for *some* complex K,L (Theorem 6's hypothesis). This
explains (but does not repair) the round-7 negative result: the "ideal
generated by Re(Φ), Im(Φ)" is a real geometric constraint on (h1,h2,h3) given
B,C, but it is satisfied by the H_i-values of *any* complex K,L whatsoever
(not just ones satisfying the geometric containment/positivity hypotheses) —
so it is far too weak to pin down Rem=0, which needs the specific 1-parameter
real family (real, positive h1,h2,h3 arising from the *unsquared*, correctly
oriented, contained K,L). Positivity alone (h1,h2,h3>0) is an inequality, not
capturable by a Gröbner-basis ideal-membership test — consistent with the
whole population's recurring diagnosis that positivity/orientation, not mere
realness/algebra, is where the missing content lives.

**3. Attempted an independent numeric replication of "Rem=0 on the true
branch" (own fsolve script, own rotation-ray parametrization, not copying the
file's numerics) — inconclusive / did not cleanly reproduce.** Using K on ray
from B at angle −θ from BA and L on ray from C at angle +θ from CA (θ the
shared hypothesis-1 angle), solving hypotheses 2,3 via `scipy.optimize.fsolve`
for (t1,s2): out of 6 random trials, 3 failed to converge, and the one
"successful" trial gave h3 with a non-negligible imaginary part (~6×10⁻⁴, not
machine-precision-real) and **Rem ≈ −3.12, not zero**. This is very likely a
sign/branch bug in my quick replication (rotation direction or angle-wrapping
convention), not a refutation of the file's claim — but it means **the file's
own round-7 confirmation of Rem=0 on the true branch rests on a thin base (3
successful configurations out of 5 attempted, with the same fsolve-convergence
fragility I just hit)**. This is worth flagging: before investing more
analytic effort proving Rem=0, next round should get a large (100+),
carefully-validated, independently-coded numeric sweep confirming Rem≈0 stays
near machine precision (not just at isolated points but as θ varies
continuously) on the genuine geometric branch — the current evidence base is
weaker than the "3-round-plateau-breaking" framing suggests.

**4. Is Rem=0 actually a smaller/different problem, or the same one repackaged?**
Important structural point for the outliner: Theorem 6 (Φ=0) is a *tautology*
for any K,L (not a constraint from the geometric hypotheses). The real content
forcing Rem=0 must come from substituting the **actual formulas** of h2(θ),
h3(θ) as rational functions of the shared free parameter θ (or equivalently
the coordinate route's u,t1,s2) — i.e. the same kind of explicit
parametrization-and-elimination that `coordinate-bash-resultant-boundary`
already built (G2a,G3a etc.), just expressed in different variables. There is
no evidence this round that Rem=0 is *easier* to establish than the other
routes' branch-selection targets — it is best understood as **the same
underlying branch-selection fact, in a genuinely smaller-dimensional package**
(3 real scalars h1,h2,h3, degree 2 in each, vs. 4 real coordinates in the
other routes) rather than a mechanism orthogonal enough to sidestep the
population's core difficulty. The "zero root-counting content" framing from
round 7 is correct for *deriving* χ=−D0/D1 (that part is genuinely linear
algebra, done), but *closing* Rem=0 will very likely require reintroducing
the θ-parametrization and hence some elimination/sign argument, structurally
similar in kind to the other three routes' remaining gaps.
