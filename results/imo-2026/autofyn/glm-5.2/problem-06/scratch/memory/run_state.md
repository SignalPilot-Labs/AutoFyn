## Goal
Solve IMO 2026 Problem 6 (problem_id `imo-2026-06`, hard, number_theory, proof_only).

Statement: Let $a_1,a_2,\ldots$ be an infinite sequence of integers $>1$. For all $n$, $a_{n+1}$ is the smallest integer $>a_n$ such that $\gcd(a_{n+1},a_i)>1$ for every $i\le n$. Prove there exist positive integers $T,L$ with $a_{n+T}=a_n+L$ for every $n$ (the sequence is eventually arithmetic up to a period).

Empirical context (computed round 1, for subagent orientation): behavior depends on $a_1$. For prime / single-prime-power-smooth starts the sequence collapses quickly to multiples of one prime ($T=1$). Non-trivial: $a_1=15=3\cdot5$ gives 15,18,20,24,30,36,40,42,45,... eventually periodic differences [3,3,2,4,6,6,4,2] (sum 30), so $T=8, L=30$.
