# Corrected determinant-residual lemma

Let
\[
K=(1-rc,rh),\qquad
L=q((1-vc)x+vhy,(1-vc)y-vhx),
\]
where `c^2+h^2=x^2+y^2=1`, and let `C-B=(qx-1,qy)`. Define
\[
R=2(|K|^2[C-B,L]+|L|^2[K,C-B])-(q^2-1)[K,L].
\]
Then
\[
R=q(P_0+qP_1+q^2P_2),
\]
where
\[
\begin{aligned}
P_0={}&-2c^2rvy-2chrvx+2cr^2vy+3cry+cvy+2hr^2vx\\
&-hrx+hvx-2r^2y-rvy-y,\\
P_1={}&-2h(r-v)(rv-1),\\
P_2={}&2c^2rvy+2chrvx-2crv^2y-cry-3cvy-2hrv^2x\\
&-hrx+hvx+rvy+2v^2y+y.
\end{aligned}
\]

This is certified by expanding the five norms/determinants and reducing only with the two displayed unit-circle identities.
