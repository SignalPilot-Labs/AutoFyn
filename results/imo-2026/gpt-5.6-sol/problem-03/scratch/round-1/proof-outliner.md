## imo-2026-03

dyadic-reserve-induction: new
Target: For every positive integer n, determine the largest guaranteed share and prove that it is c_n = 2^n/(2^{n+1}-1), by giving Liu Bang a legal marking strategy attaining this value and giving Xiang Yu a matching response against every Liu marking.
Technique: Greedy drafting reduction followed by strengthened induction with a dyadic reserve invariant (knowledge-base entries: Reformulate; Induction with a strengthened hypothesis; Invariants & monovariants; Constructive vs. existence). This adapts the reserve-buffer move of corpus problem aimo-0340 and the dominant-scale idea of aimo-0117, but both refinement lemmas must be proved here from scratch.
Skeleton:
  1. For any fixed final multiset b_1 >= ... >= b_m of piece lengths, prove by backward induction/exchange that optimal play takes a longest remaining piece, so Liu's value is P(b)=b_1+b_3+... — by greedy exchange for alternating selection.
  2. Append a zero if needed and define the sorted alternating discrepancy D(b)=b_1-b_2+b_3-b_4+...; then P(b)=(1+D(b))/2 — by separating total sum and alternating sum.
  3. Liu uses all n marks to make initial gaps 1,2,4,...,2^n divided by Q=2^{n+1}-1 — by an explicit legal construction with distinct cumulative-sum marks.
  4. Prove the dyadic reserve lemma: refining the multiset {1,2,...,2^n} by at most n binary cuts leaves D at least 1; conclude P >= (Q+1)/(2Q)=2^n/Q — by induction on the number of scales and cuts, retaining one unit of unmatched reserve after adjacent sorted cancellation.
  5. Prove the universal refinement lemma: for any at most n+1 positive parent lengths of total 1, Xiang can make at most n cuts so that the final sorted discrepancy is at most 1/Q (or, with legal positive/distinct cuts, at most 1/Q+epsilon for every epsilon>0) — by a strengthened induction that tracks total mass together with one unmatched boundary fragment and uses the cut budget as the reserve counter.
  6. Convert the universal lemma to P <= 2^n/Q (or P <= 2^n/Q+epsilon/2); hence no number larger than 2^n/Q is guaranteed — by the discrepancy identity and the definition of supremal guarantee.
  7. Check n=1 directly: Liu's gaps 1/3,2/3 guarantee 2/3, while the universal lemma specializes to one explicit split of arbitrary two parent gaps; this verifies the formula's base case and equality normalization.
Key lemmas (claim + the one-line mechanism that makes it true):
  - Greedy drafting lemma: the first player receives the odd-ranked lengths under optimal play — because taking a nonmaximum piece can be exchanged with the earliest later taking of a maximum without decreasing the mover's total, and backward induction applies after its removal.
  - Dyadic reserve lemma: if {1,2,...,2^n} is subjected to at most n splits, its sorted alternating discrepancy is at least 1 — because adjacent cancellation can be organized scale by scale, and every split that destroys one dominant dyadic block consumes one unit of the cut budget while passing an unmatched reserve to lower scales; after n cuts one unit remains.
  - Universal refinement lemma: every collection of at most n+1 parent masses totaling S admits n refinements with D <= S/(2^{n+1}-1), in the exact or arbitrarily approximating sense appropriate to positive cuts — because the induction chooses a threshold fragment that brackets the current unmatched discrepancy, cuts at that threshold, and rescales the two residual states whose coefficients add in the binary relation 1+2+...+2^n.
Open gaps: Steps 4 and 5 are the load-bearing gaps. The builder must formulate the reserve state precisely enough that it is preserved under arbitrary split locations in Step 4, and must give a valid cut-selection induction in Step 5 rather than merely assert balancing. Step 1 also needs a short rigorous minimax/exchange argument in the presence of ties. If Step 5 yields only an infimum, Step 6 must phrase the epsilon argument carefully.
Cases to cover: Liu uses r<n marks (hence r+1<n+1 parent intervals) versus all n; Xiang uses fewer than n cuts; odd versus even final piece count (append a zero only algebraically); tied lengths; cut locations tending to endpoints versus legal interior distinct marks; base case n=1.
Watch out for: Do not assume cuts lie in different Liu intervals or that Xiang can repartition total mass without provenance. Do not silently treat zero-length pieces as legal. The explorer's ternary candidate is rejected: for n=2 its guarantee 5/9 is already below the dyadic construction's tested/proposed 4/7, so it cannot be the optimum. Equal initial spacing is also known to collapse toward 1/2.

threshold-parity-toggles: new
Target: For every positive integer n, prove end to end that the largest guaranteed share is c_n = 2^n/(2^{n+1}-1), including both the dyadic Liu construction and a matching Xiang strategy for arbitrary initial marks.
Technique: Layer-cake representation of alternating order statistics and parity-set toggling (knowledge-base entries: Reformulate; Invariants & monovariants; Double counting; Induction; Constructive vs. existence). This route avoids rank-by-rank reserve cancellation and treats each cut as a geometric toggle in threshold space.
Skeleton:
  1. Reduce optimal claiming to the odd-rank payoff P and discrepancy D exactly as in the greedy drafting lemma, with all tie cases justified.
  2. For N_b(t)=#{i:b_i>=t}, prove D(b)=integral_0^infinity [N_b(t) is odd] dt — by writing each b_i as the integral of 1_{t<=b_i} and summing alternating rank indicators.
  3. Encode a multiset by its odd-threshold set E(b)={t:N_b(t) is odd}; then D(b)=|E(b)|.
  4. Calculate exactly how one cut x=u+v (u<=v) changes E: its symmetric difference with the old E is the union (0,u] union (v,x], up to endpoints, of total measure 2u — by comparing the parity contribution of {x} with that of {u,v} at every threshold.
  5. For Liu's dyadic initial lengths 1,2,...,2^n, prove that after at most n admissible toggle operations of the special form in Step 4, the resulting odd set has measure at least 1 — by a scale-induction parity lemma on the nested endpoints 1,2,4,...,2^n.
  6. For arbitrary at most n+1 initial lengths summing to Q after scaling, prove Xiang can choose at most n special toggles so the final odd set has measure at most 1 (or at most 1+epsilon with legal cuts) — by choosing toggle endpoints at successive boundary points of E and charging each surviving odd component to a distinct binary-scale mass; if more than unit measure survives, the charged parents would total more than Q.
  7. Rescale Steps 5 and 6 and use P=(1+D)/2 to obtain the lower and upper bounds 2^n/(2^{n+1}-1); verify the explicit marks and the epsilon/legal-cut passage.
Key lemmas (claim + the one-line mechanism that makes it true):
  - Layer-cake identity: D is the measure of the thresholds at which an odd number of pieces survive — because at a fixed threshold the alternating sum of the first N_b(t) unit indicators is exactly N_b(t) mod 2.
  - Single-cut toggle identity: splitting x into u<=v toggles parity precisely on (0,u] and (v,u+v] — because below u the piece count changes from one to two, between u and v it remains one, and between v and u+v it changes from one to zero.
  - Dyadic parity lemma: n such two-interval toggles cannot erase all but less than one unit of the initial dyadic odd-threshold set — because crossing each dyadic boundary changes the available parity reserve, while a toggle capable of repairing that crossing is charged to one of only n cuts.
  - Universal parity-covering lemma: from any n+1 parent thresholds of total Q, n legal toggles can leave odd measure at most 1 — because a greedy boundary cancellation creates disjoint charged layers with weights at least 1,2,...,2^n if residual odd measure exceeded 1, contradicting total Q.
Open gaps: Steps 5 and 6 are explicit hard gaps. The builder must prove the claimed dyadic boundary induction and the greedy parity-covering/charging lemma, including why every selected abstract toggle corresponds to splitting one existing parent rather than an arbitrary parity operation. Step 4 is elementary but must handle endpoint conventions only up to measure zero.
Cases to cover: Fewer than n initial marks; fewer than n Xiang cuts; repeated/tied lengths; a parent split more than once (the toggle must then be applied to a current daughter, not falsely to the original parent); endpoint-limit cuts; n=1.
Watch out for: Symmetric difference does not imply that every toggle reduces |E|; the overlap signs must be controlled in Step 6. Physical adjacency within a parent is irrelevant to daughter lengths, but provenance is not. A parity argument that permits arbitrary unions of two intervals is too strong unless realizability by cuts is proved.

adjacent-matching-hall: new
Target: For every positive integer n, establish the exact value c_n = 2^n/(2^{n+1}-1) through adjacent matching of sorted final pieces, with an attaining Liu construction and a universal Xiang response.
Technique: Sorted adjacent matching, charging/injection, and Hall's marriage theorem (knowledge-base entries: Hall's marriage theorem / SDR; Pigeonhole / extremal principle; Reformulate; Constructive vs. existence). The borrowed intuition is the fixed-pair exceptional-reserve move of aimo-0596, but no XOR or game-specific assertion is imported.
Skeleton:
  1. Prove the greedy drafting reduction and write D=(b_1-b_2)+(b_3-b_4)+... after appending a zero if required; thus D is the total cost of the canonical adjacent matching in descending order.
  2. Give Liu the normalized dyadic parent lengths. Color every final daughter by its dyadic parent and pair adjacent sorted daughters.
  3. Build a bipartite charging graph from all positive pair gaps to split events/scales, with one exceptional unit vertex; prove Hall's condition and inject every unit of gap cancellation into available lower-scale mass, leaving at least one unmatched unit — by the strict prefix dominance 2^j=1+sum_{i<j}2^i and the fact that only n cuts create additional daughters.
  4. Deduce D>=1/Q and hence Liu's guarantee.
  5. For arbitrary parent masses, construct Xiang's cuts and a near-perfect adjacent matching simultaneously: repeatedly select the largest currently unmatched parent remainder and cut off a fragment equal to a neighboring unmatched amount, so that all but residual total at most 1/Q can be paired with equal or nearly equal partners — by a greedy matching process whose failure certificate would be a Hall-deficient set.
  6. Prove that any Hall-deficient set in Step 5 forces n+1 parent masses to dominate successive binary partial sums totaling more than 1; this contradiction establishes a refinement with matching cost D<=1/Q (or +epsilon).
  7. Translate to payoff, handle legal perturbations, and state/verify c_n.
Key lemmas (claim + the one-line mechanism that makes it true):
  - Adjacent matching identity: D is the sum of excesses in the descending adjacent pairs — because the sorted alternating sum groups exactly as (b_1-b_2)+(b_3-b_4)+....
  - Dyadic Hall lemma: under at most n cuts of dyadic parents, all cancellation demands except one unit admit distinct lower-scale charges — because a subset whose demands outnumber charges would require more daughter-creating cuts than the subset's number of crossed scales.
  - Refinement matching lemma: arbitrary n+1 parent masses can be refined with n cuts so the canonical adjacent matching has total excess at most 1/Q — because equal-fragment greedy pairing either succeeds or yields a Hall-deficiency chain whose masses grow at least by factors encoded by 1+2+...+2^n, impossible at total mass 1.
Open gaps: Steps 3, 5, and 6 are substantial. In particular, the graph's vertices and continuous capacities must be defined precisely (ordinary Hall handles discrete vertices, so the builder may need to discretize at breakpoints or prove a capacitated Hall/max-flow variant from scratch). The greedy equal-fragment process must respect at most n cuts and must imply control of the canonical sorted matching, not merely some arbitrary matching.
Cases to cover: A final odd number of positive pieces and the dummy zero; equality/ties in sorted order; multiple cuts in one parent; parents already exactly matchable; fewer parent intervals; epsilon perturbation for zero-size limiting fragments.
Watch out for: Existence of a low-cost arbitrary matching does not automatically bound the canonical adjacent matching unless one proves the uncrossing fact that sorted adjacent matching minimizes total absolute pair difference. Continuous mass cannot be inserted into a finite Hall graph without a justified capacitated formulation.

polyhedral-minimax-certificate: new
Target: For every positive integer n, compute and prove the exact game value c_n = 2^n/(2^{n+1}-1), certifying both players' strategies through the finite piecewise-linear minimax problem.
Technique: Polyhedral decomposition, linear-programming dual certificates, and induction on the number of cuts/order chambers (knowledge-base entries: Extreme value theorem; Standard inequalities and equality cases; Casework / exhaustion; Induction; Reformulate; Constructive vs. existence). This is the heavier fallback after the cheap greedy drafting reduction; it is justified only if the structural reserve lemmas resist direct proof.
Skeleton:
  1. Reduce the drafting stage to P=(1+D)/2 and parameterize Liu by a simplex of at most n+1 parent lengths and Xiang by a finite cut-allocation vector plus daughter lengths summing to each parent.
  2. For each cut allocation and weak ordering of all daughters, express D as a linear form subject to linear inequalities; Xiang's best reply in that chamber is therefore an LP.
  3. Prove a chamber-compression lemma: it suffices to retain only recursively nested order types in which a cut daughter meets an existing breakpoint or tends to zero — by moving a cut linearly inside a chamber until a boundary without increasing the linear objective.
  4. Solve the resulting family recursively and exhibit dual multipliers whose objective is 1/Q for every Liu parent vector; the multipliers form a binary tree and sum according to Q=1+2+...+2^n — by LP weak duality plus induction on the compressed chamber tree.
  5. At the dyadic Liu vector, exhibit primal/dual complementary slackness showing every Xiang refinement has D>=1/Q — by a dual certificate whose coefficients are the dyadic scale reserves.
  6. Convert the two certificates to c_n, and replace any zero daughter in an extremal LP by a sufficiently small positive perturbation to obtain legal distinct cut points and the required epsilon upper response.
Key lemmas (claim + the one-line mechanism that makes it true):
  - Finite chamber lemma: after fixing cut counts, there are finitely many daughter orderings and D is linear on each closed ordering chamber — because sorting only chooses signs/ranks and every provenance/sum constraint is linear.
  - Boundary compression lemma: a linear objective over a compact polytope attains an extremum at a face/vertex where daughter lengths collide, vanish, or a provenance sum constraint is tight — because of the extreme-point theorem for linear programming.
  - Uniform binary dual certificate: across every compressed chamber, nonnegative combinations of ordering and parent-sum constraints yield D<=1/Q for a suitable Xiang primal response, while at the dyadic vector the reverse certificate yields D>=1/Q — because the recursive multipliers telescope with coefficients 1,2,...,2^n.
Open gaps: Steps 3-5 are hard and currently unproved. The builder must not claim that checking one chamber or numerical LP suffices; it needs a symbolic recursive classification and explicit dual multipliers valid for arbitrary n. Compactness also needs care because legal cuts require strict positivity; work first in the closure and then perturb only for the upper-bound response.
Cases to cover: Every composition of at most n cuts among at most n+1 parents; all weak orderings including ties; boundary points with zero daughters; fewer marks/cuts; n=1 base chamber; legality/distinctness after perturbation.
Watch out for: The number of chambers is finite for fixed n but not a proof for all n without the compression recurrence. LP duality orientation must be checked: Xiang minimizes D for fixed parents, whereas Liu maximizes the resulting value. A certificate discovered computationally is not rigorous until its formulas and nonnegativity are proved for every n.
