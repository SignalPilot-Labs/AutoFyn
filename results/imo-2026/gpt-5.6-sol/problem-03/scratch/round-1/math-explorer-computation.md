- Distinct openings:
  1. **Geometric initial partition / alternating-rank payoff.** Once all marks are placed, optimal drafting is completely determined by sorting the final piece lengths: Liu Bang receives ranks `1,3,5,…`, so his payoff is `x_(1)+x_(3)+…`. Computation strongly points to Liu marking so that his `n+1` initial intervals, in increasing order, have lengths
     `3^{-n}, 2·3^{-n}, 2·3^{-(n-1)}, …, 2/3` (the order along the stick can be arbitrary). For this partition, continuous reply optimization for `n=1,2,3,4,5` returned respectively `2/3, 5/9, 14/27, 41/81, 122/243`, i.e. `1/2 + 1/(2·3^n)`. This is the strongest answer conjecture.
  2. **Threshold/counting reformulation.** For final lengths `y_1,…,y_{2n+1}`, the odd-rank sum can be studied through the count `N(t)=#{i:y_i≥t}` (or by pairing ranks 2–3, 4–5, etc.). Xiang's operation is exactly refinement of Liu's `n+1` initial intervals using at most `n` new cuts. This suggests a whole-problem route phrased as controlling alternating order statistics under refinement, rather than tracking play move by move.
  3. **Ternary charging / superincreasing route.** In the conjectured construction, each interval after the smallest equals twice the sum of all preceding intervals: if `a_0=3^{-n}` and `a_j=2·3^{j-1-n}`, then `a_j=2(a_0+…+a_{j-1})`. This exact dominance relation is likely the structural reason for the base `3`; it permits charging smaller pieces against a split larger interval. This is distinct from a generic minimax calculation and is a plausible end-to-end constructive route.
  4. **Adversarial recursive splitting route for the upper bound.** To show no initial marking beats the conjectured value, computation suggests Xiang should repeatedly act on a suitably dominant Liu interval, using cuts to create equal or threshold-matched pieces. The numerical optimizer frequently puts several cuts in one large interval and lets one nominal fragment tend to zero, so a clean upper-bound formulation may use an infimum/limit response first and then perturb cuts to make all marked points distinct. This route must explicitly handle that Xiang may mark *at most* `n` points and that zero-length limiting fragments are not legal.
  5. **Finite piecewise-linear minimax route.** For fixed numbers of Xiang cuts assigned among Liu's intervals and a fixed ordering of all resulting fragment lengths, Xiang's minimization is a linear program. Thus the continuous game is a finite collection of LP regions. Symbolic LP dual certificates could discover the correct inequality and equality pattern for the conjectured ternary partition, without relying on numerical calculus.
