# Envelope exactification lemma

Under the hypotheses of the one-orbit tail-envelope lemma, every positive value of \(g\) equals \(c\).

## Proof

Suppose \(g(u)=a>0\). By the arithmetic-orbit lemma,
\[
t_n=u+na,\qquad g(t_n)=a
\]
for all integers \(n\ge0\). Since \(t_n\to\infty\), the tail-envelope estimate applies for all sufficiently large \(n\), giving
\[
|a-c|=|g(t_n)-c|\le \frac{c^2}{8t_n}.
\]
The right side tends to zero, so \(|a-c|=0\), i.e. \(a=c\). ∎
